# 简体中文: style guide

File `zh_Hans.po`, glossary key `zh-Hans`. Read [the shared guide](README.md) first.

## Address and register

- 你 between characters and in hints, not 您.
- UI and buttons: short (继续, 设置).

## The engineer

- Never 他 or 她 for the engineer: use the name or drop the pronoun.
- No gendered person words or honorifics (小姐, 女士, 先生).

## WOLF

WOLF is **他**, never 它: he is a person, not an object. Calm and dry.

## Names

Stored per language (D9): 罗温, 亚历克斯, 摩根 (samples in `../glossary.json`).

## Quotes and punctuation

- Quotes “…”.
- Keep 。 and ， in the game's boxes.
- Reading speed is slower than English (9 characters a second in Netflix's subtitle guide).

## Open questions

- **TANK RELEASE**: 储罐投放 is a proposal.
- **the Director**: 所长 follows the Japanese 所長; title or name is still open.
- **SERVICE JUNCTION**: a reviewer's word.
