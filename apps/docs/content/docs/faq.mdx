**What this page is for:** short, spoiler-free answers, and a glossary of the game's words.

## Playing

### Can I control WOLF?

- **No.** You always play the engineer.
- **WOLF is an independent companion.** He moves, helps or refuses on his own.
- **You can ask him things.** Your answers change what he says and does.

### What if WOLF refuses to help?

- **You can always go on.** Every essential step has a way the engineer can do alone.
- **It may take longer,** but nothing is lost.

### Can I fail for good?

- **No.** A knockdown sends you back to the start of the step you were on.
- **Your choices are kept.** See [Knockdowns](/docs/controls/saves/#knockdowns).

### Do I have to answer quickly?

- **No.** Questions have no timer. They wait until you answer.

### Is there flashing?

- **Yes, some.** Red flashes on a knockdown, flashing beacons in the corridor and a flashing door lamp in one room.
- **REDUCED MOTION** shortens flashes and holds the lamps steady. See [Accessibility](/docs/accessibility/#reduced-motion).

### How long is it?

- **Not measured yet.** Chapter 1 has three puzzle rooms and a maintenance annex. No complete route has been played by hand and timed.

### How do I save?

- **Use the green SAFE POINT** in the first room. After that, the game saves on its own. See [Saving and retrying](/docs/controls/saves/).

### Is there music or voice acting?

- **Not yet.** There are sound effects only.

### What languages does it support?

- **English, plus German, Spanish, French, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) as machine translations** of the menus, the HUD and the story, not yet reviewed by a native speaker. More languages are planned. See [Platforms and languages](/docs/platforms/#languages).

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **The engineer** | The character you play. |
| **WOLF** | The robotic wolf who travels with you. He acts on his own. |
| **THE DEN** | The facility where the game takes place. |
| **Station** | A labelled machine or panel you can use. A prompt shows when you are close enough. |
| **Interact** | The use button: **E**, controller **A**, or **USE** on touch. |
| **Prompt** / **hint line** | The short line that says which button to press, or what to do next. |
| **Objective card** | A card that shows briefly when your goal changes. |
| **Crank** | Turning a wheel by holding interact. See [Hold to crank](/docs/controls/input/#hold-to-crank). |
| **SAFE POINT** | The green station that makes your first save. |
| **Checkpoint** | Your one save. Each new save replaces it. |
| **Knockdown** | When a hazard knocks the engineer to the floor. Press **RETRY** to go back one step. |
| **Slice** | The current playable part of the game, ending after the maintenance annex. |
| **Source build** | The game run from its source code with Godot, the free game engine it is made in. |
| **Test build** | An automatic, unsigned build for testing, made for macOS and Windows. |
