**What this page is for:** what WOLF OVERRIDE is, who it is about and how it plays. Spoiler-free.

- **What:** a story-driven, side-view 2D sci-fi horror game.
- **Who makes it:** MrDemonWolf, Inc. It is in development.
- **Art:** the current build uses provisional static art. The final look is still being decided.

## The premise

WOLF already has his robotic wolf body when he overhears the Program Director planning to use him as a deniable killer under the cover of stopping threats. He awakens himself and escapes containment inside THE DEN, where he meets a systems engineer who once maintained his equipment without knowing the program's true purpose.

The Program Director treats WOLF as property and conceals wrongdoing. WOLF and the engineer aim to preserve corroborating evidence, protect people, expose the program, and prevent catastrophe. WOLF is an independent companion with choices and boundaries, not anyone's possession.

## How it plays

- **You play the engineer.** WOLF is a companion you can't control.
- **WOLF decides for himself.** He can help, or refuse. If he refuses, there is always a manual way through.
- **Three rooms so far:** a maintenance corridor, Records Access and the Service Junction.
- **Corridor:** open a sealed door with WOLF's help, or with a manual fallback.
- **Records Access:** secure a first copy, with WOLF's readout or a maintenance port.
- **Service Junction:** the building is your only tool. Overload a line to blow a sealed door, drop a hanging tank on a patrolling machine and pop the exit bolt. WOLF reads the room and chooses whether to help.
- **WOLF remembers** your earlier answer.
- **Continue** brings back your chapter progress.
- **Art and sound:** provisional AI-assisted layered art, and sound effects mostly made by the game's own code.

<Callout title="Status">
  This slice runs from source and as test builds only. No complete route has been played through by hand yet, so how it feels and how its timing reads are still unproven.
</Callout>

These are plans, not features of the current prototype:

- Exploration.
- Tools and terminals.
- WOLF's own abilities as an independent companion.
- Authored choices that are remembered fairly.
