## Start playing

1. Get the game: a [test build](/docs/test-builds/) or the source game in **Godot 4.7.2**. [Get the game](/docs/get-game/) has the steps.
2. Start it.
3. Choose **New Game**. Advance the opening at your pace, then follow the movement and interaction prompts.

- The opening shows WOLF and the Director before the engineer enters.
- **E** advances a beat. **Esc** pauses, and Pause offers **Skip Opening**.
- These are default keys. [Controls](/docs/controls/input/) lists the controller and touch equivalents.
- **First time playing?** Follow the spoiler-light [First steps tutorial](/docs/controls/first-steps/) for movement, interaction prompts, choices, and checkpoints.

## Your first goal

- **Goal:** restore power and open the sealed maintenance door.
- Read the station labels and the nearby interaction prompt.
- Objective cards appear briefly when your goal changes.
- WOLF can help or refuse. You keep control of the engineer, and a manual route lets you continue if he declines.
- A choice you make in the corridor is remembered later.
- **Stuck?** The [optional chapter walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/) separates the corridor, Records Access and Service Junction solutions. It reveals only the current source chapter.

## Save your progress

- Use the green **SAFE POINT** beyond the door.
- Records Access and the Service Junction then save automatically at key moments.
- **Continue** on the title screen resumes a valid checkpoint without replaying the opening.
- [Saving](/docs/controls/saves/) explains Return to Title, New Game, and where the single checkpoint file lives.

## What to expect

- Characters and environments use provisional artwork.
- Final animation, sound, the wider campaign, and release packages are still in development.
- The current chapter is a small beginning, and its puzzles do not select a campaign ending.
- [What was checked](/docs/development/evidence/) lists what has actually been checked, including that complete routes have not been played by hand.
