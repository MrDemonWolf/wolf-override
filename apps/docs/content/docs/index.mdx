**You play the engineer.** WOLF follows and takes a task of his own. In this source build, open the first sealed door, then secure a first copy in Records Access. WOLF remembers what you say during the door puzzle.

**Your path:** Breaker → Relay → Safe point → Purge queue → Mirror port → Exit. A short objective card appears when your goal changes, then fades. Follow the station labels and the prompt near the bottom if you lose your place.

This game currently runs from Godot source. The website is a guide; there is no browser game or downloadable build yet.

## 1. Start New Game

**Already have the repository?** With [Godot **4.7.2 stable**](https://godotengine.org/download/archive/) installed, run this from the repository root:

```sh
godot --path apps/game
```

**Need the source first?** Clone it, then launch the game:

```sh
git clone https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override.git
cd wolf-override
godot --path apps/game
```

Choose **New Game** on the title screen. The short opening shows only WOLF and the Director as WOLF wakes himself and leaves containment. Press **E** to advance each beat, or **Esc** to skip to engineer control. A small prompt then teaches movement and reading the first display. Continue resumes your save without replaying the opening.

## 2. Use the breaker

Move right to the **amber BREAKER**. Press **E** to hear WOLF's concern about the coolant risk. Press **1** or **2** to answer, then press **E** again to arm power.

Your answer changes how WOLF responds at the relay. It is remembered at the safe point; it is not an instant game ending.

## 3. Open the door at the relay

Continue right to the **blue COOLANT RELAY**.

- **If you chose 1 (tell WOLF the risk):** Stand toward the right side of the relay so WOLF can reach its contact, then press **E**. If he asks for room, move a few steps right while the relay prompt stays visible and press **E** again.
- **If you chose 2 (press WOLF to act):** Press **E** to hear his refusal. Press **E** again to use the engineer's manual bypass. You can still open the door.

## 4. Save at the safe point

Walk through the open door to the **green SAFE POINT** and press **E**. WOLF recalls the answer you actually chose. The checkpoint saves both characters' positions, door state, name, and choice. Press **E** again to enter **Records Access**.

On the title screen, **Continue** becomes available when a valid checkpoint exists. During play, **L** or **F9** loads your saved progress. **N** starts a clean current run; it does not delete the save file.

## 5. Secure the first copy

In Records Access, the engineer stays playable while WOLF heads toward the mirror on his own.

1. Go to the **purge queue** on the left and press **E** to copy its trace.
2. Go to the **mirror port** in the middle and press **E**. Press **1** after WOLF arrives to use his readout, or **2** to use the engineer's manual maintenance port. If WOLF is still checking, wait for him or choose the manual port.
3. Go to the **exit** on the right and press **E**. The shutter opens as the first copy is secured. Press **E** once more to return to control. **Continue** restores the room and your progress.

These two traces provide a first lead. They are not a complete case against the Director.

## Keys to remember

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or arrow keys | Move the engineer |
| E | Advance the opening or interact with a station |
| 1 / 2 or click a response | Answer at the breaker or mirror port |
| Esc | Pause or resume during play; skip the opening |

**Controller:** Left stick or D-pad moves. A interacts, X/Y picks choices 1/2, and Start pauses. This mapping has an automated check; physical controller input still needs a playtest.

**Touch:** On iOS and Android, hold the left/right arrows to move, tap **USE** to interact, and tap **1** or **2** for a choice. Tap **PAUSE** for Resume, Settings, or Return to Title. From the title, choose New Game or Continue from your saved checkpoint. Returning to title keeps your checkpoint but loses unsaved progress. Touch gestures still need a hands-on device or simulator playtest.

**Settings:** Open Settings from the title screen or pause menu. Display includes 30/60/90/120/144 FPS or uncapped, V-sync, and 2D edge smoothing. Desktop also has window sizes and fullscreen; mobile window size is OS-managed. Audio has Master, Music, Effects, and Voice volume. Controls lets you remap movement, interaction, choices, and pause for keyboard and controller, adjust stick deadzone, or restore defaults. Preferences are saved. Audio tracks are still being developed; physical controller input remains untested.

### More keys

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| I | Cycle provisional engineer display names |
| L or F9 | Load the saved checkpoint |
| N | Restart the current run |

At the far-left **red PURGE DISPLAY**, **E** or **USE** shows optional story context. It is not required to open the door.

## What exists today

The first chapter slice has two playable rooms with provisional static character art. [About the game](/docs/about/) explains the wider premise; [development status](/docs/development/) separates built features from plans.

Godot 4.7.2 headless import, state, M0 scene, chapter scene, and input/settings checks passed. The purge interaction was checked in a normal Godot window, and tutorial, Records completion, touch layout, and pause/settings frames were inspected at 960×540. The full routes have not been played by hand. A local iOS simulator build launched on two simulators and its title screen was inspected on iPhone 17e. Full touch play, controller hardware, physical devices, and public export packages remain open.
