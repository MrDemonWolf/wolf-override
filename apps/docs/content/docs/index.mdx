**Play the engineer. Get through THE DEN with WOLF.** Explore a side-view facility, solve a path forward, and choose how you respond to an independent robotic companion.

The current source chapter has two rooms, an opening scene, a cooperation or manual door puzzle, a remembered disagreement, and checkpoints. This website is the guide.

## Get the game

**Status:** there is no packaged installer or public release yet. You can try the current development build by downloading its source and opening it with Godot 4.7.2; the source archive is a development snapshot, not a ready-to-run app.

## Start playing

1. Follow [source setup](/docs/development/setup/) to get the source and **Godot 4.7.2**.
2. From the repository root, launch the imported project:

   ```sh
   godot --path apps/game
   ```

3. Choose **New Game**. Advance the opening at your pace, then follow the movement and interaction prompts.

The opening shows WOLF and the Director before the engineer enters. **E** advances a beat; **Esc** pauses, and Pause offers **Skip Opening**. These are default keys; the [Input](/docs/controls/input/) page lists the controller and touch equivalents.

**First time playing?** Follow the spoiler-light [First steps tutorial](/docs/controls/first-steps/) for movement, interaction prompts, choices, and checkpoints.

## Your first goal

Restore power and open the sealed maintenance door. Read the station labels and the nearby interaction prompt. Objective cards appear briefly when your goal changes.

WOLF can help or refuse. You keep control of the engineer, and a manual route lets you continue if he declines. A choice you make in the corridor is remembered later.

**Stuck?** The [optional chapter walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/) separates the corridor and Records Access solutions. It reveals only the current source chapter.

## Save your progress

Use the green **SAFE POINT** beyond the door; the stations in the second room then save automatically. **Continue** on the title screen resumes a valid checkpoint without replaying the opening. [Saving](/docs/controls/saves/) explains Return to Title, New Game, and where the single checkpoint file lives.

## What to expect

Characters and environments use provisional artwork. Final animation, sound, the wider campaign, and release packages are still in development. The current chapter is a small beginning, and its puzzles do not select a campaign ending. [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/) lists what has actually been checked, including that complete routes have not been played by hand.
