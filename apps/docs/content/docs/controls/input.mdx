**What this page is for:** every action on keyboard, controller and touch, in one table.

- **You control the engineer.** WOLF is an independent companion.
- **These are the defaults.** In-game prompts always show your current bindings.
- **Few buttons.** Every room uses the same small set: move, interact, the two answers and pause. Objective recall is optional.

## Switching inputs

- A tap enables touch controls on desktop builds too.
- Keyboard and controller presses switch away from touch controls.
- Mouse movement alone keeps the current prompts.
- On-screen **PAUSE** follows touch controls; keyboard and controller keep their mapped pause action.

## If a controller disconnects

- Losing the controller in use pauses play and releases held actions.
- Reconnecting keeps the game paused until you choose **RESUME**.
- Keyboard and touch can also resume play.

## Leave the game

1. Open Pause and choose **RETURN TO TITLE**.
2. On a desktop or handheld PC, choose **QUIT** at the end of the title menu.

- Phones and web builds use their system controls to leave the app or page.
- Progress resumes from the last checkpoint; see [Saving and retrying](/docs/controls/saves/).

## Hold to crank

**Crank** means turning a wheel by holding interact. Two wheels need it: the corridor's **MANUAL BYPASS** and the Service Junction's **VALVE**.

1. Walk up to the wheel. The prompt says **HOLD**.
2. Hold interact (**E**, controller **A**, or the action pill on touch).
3. Keep holding until it is done.

- **It starts at once.** The wheel gives a little with a soft click the moment you press.
- **Turns you made stay done** if you let go, pause or step away.
- **Can't hold a button?** Turn on **TOGGLE HOLDS** (below).

### TOGGLE HOLDS

- **Where:** [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#controls), Controls tab. Off by default.
- **What it does:** one press starts a crank; it keeps turning on its own.
- **To stop:** press interact again, walk away or pause. It also stops when the crank is done.
- **Works on** keyboard, controller and touch, because all three press the same interact action.
- **Prompts change:** they drop the word **HOLD** (for example **E: crank the valve**).
- **It is saved** with your other preferences. **RESTORE DEFAULT CONTROLS** does not change it.

<Details>
<Summary>Crank timing</Summary>

- **VALVE:** each 0.4 seconds held is one turn. Three turns charge the door.
- **MANUAL BYPASS:** each quarter second held is one of eight notches, two seconds in all.

</Details>

## Read the story

- **When a conversation stops movement,** press Interact or tap dialogue to advance. Its final page returns movement.
- **Timed walk-and-talk keeps movement and USE available**, including on touch.
- **Brief speech follows its speaker** when it fits safely; otherwise it uses the lower reading area.
- **Routine hints, objectives, station names and tutorial panels hide during dialogue** and return when exploring.
- **Urgent warnings stay visible:** save failures, recovery prompts and live hazards.
- **The objective returns after dialogue clears.** It shows the current goal without a room heading, then fades.
- **Forgot the goal?** Open Pause to see it above Resume during play.
- **Question pages wait for input.** Press Interact or tap dialogue while the continue triangle shows; choose an answer when ready.

## Click or tap the room

- **Click or tap a machine** to walk up and use it through the same interaction as keyboard **E**.
- **Point at a machine out of reach** with the mouse and a small **Walk here** label appears on it; clicking walks you over.
- **Floor input:** desktop clicks walk on release. Touch starts the pad in the empty left zone; other floor taps walk on release.
- **Drag away before release** to cancel. Manual movement takes over from a queued walk.
- **A distant wheel** needs a second press when reached; walking up never starts a crank automatically.
- **Tap dialogue** to read the next page or line without activating a machine behind it.
- **Menus and touch buttons** take priority over the room beneath them.

<Callout title="Current checks">Pointer cancellation, navigation and viewport event routing have headless checks. Physical touch and mouse play remain unverified.</Callout>

## Keyboard and mouse

- **Answers appear as buttons** on screen when a choice is waiting, so you can click them.
- **Title and pause menus** take the mouse as well as the keyboard.

## Controller

- **The selected menu item** shows a cyan outline inside its border.
- **Hold a D-pad or left-stick direction** to keep moving through menu controls. Releasing it stops; changing pages or leaving the app clears the held direction.
- **Right stick** scrolls a long menu page, the credits or the changelog. **LT** and **RT** move it one page up or down.
- **Closing Settings** from the title returns the selection to **SETTINGS**.

## Touch (iOS and Android builds)

- **Move:** press an empty area on the left, then slide left or right. Objects take priority over the pad.
- **PAUSE** opens Resume, Settings and Return to Title (Skip Opening during the opening).
- **Leaving the app** during play pauses the game.
- **Android Back** steps back one menu, and pauses play. It closes the game only from the title screen.
- **Touch menus show no outline.** The first arrow key or controller press selects an item without using it.
- **Tap the action pill near a station** to use it. A **HOLD** cue means keep holding it.
- **The separate USE button hides** while the pill offers that action. It remains available for other play and recovery prompts.
- **Answers** replace movement and **USE** controls at the bottom, side by side, within thumb reach.

<Details>
<Summary>Where things sit on a touch screen</Summary>

- Dialogue and control visibility follow [Read the story](#read-the-story).
- Larger text splits into more dialogue pages inside the safe area.
- Touch answers show their words only, without the 1 and 2 of the keyboard.
- The camera sits a little lower on touch, so feet and machine bases stay above the buttons. It stays there while answers show.

</Details>
