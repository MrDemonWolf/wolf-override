**What this page is for:** every action on keyboard, controller and touch, in one table.

- **You control the engineer.** WOLF is an independent companion.
- **These are the defaults.** In-game prompts always show your current bindings.
- **Few buttons.** Every room uses the same small set: move, interact, the two answers and pause. There are no other buttons to learn.

## All actions

| Action | Keyboard | Controller | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or ← / → | Left stick or D-pad | Hold ◀ / ▶ |
| Interact; next line in the opening or closing scene | E | A | USE (reads CONTINUE in those scenes) |
| Retry after a knockdown | E | A | USE (reads RETRY) |
| Crank a wheel ([how](#hold-to-crank)) | Hold E | Hold A | Hold USE (reads CRANK) |
| Answer 1 / Answer 2 | 1 / 2, or click | X / Y | Tap the answer |
| Pause or resume | Esc | Start | PAUSE; tap RESUME |
| Back in menus | Esc | B | Android Back |
| Cancel a rebind | Esc or CANCEL REBIND | — | Android Back |
| Cycle the provisional engineer name | I (fixed) | — | — |

- **Rebind** Move, Interact, both answers and Pause in [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#controls).
- **Controller labels are generic.** Your controller may name the same buttons differently.
- **Retry timing:** see [Knockdowns](/docs/controls/saves/#knockdowns).

## Hold to crank

**Crank** means turning a wheel by holding interact. Two wheels need it: the corridor's **MANUAL BYPASS** and the Service Junction's **VALVE**.

1. Walk up to the wheel. The prompt says **HOLD**.
2. Hold interact (**E**, controller **A**, or **CRANK** on touch).
3. Keep holding until it is done.

- **It starts at once.** The wheel gives a little with a soft click the moment you press.
- **Turns you made stay done** if you let go, pause or step away.
- **Can't hold a button?** Turn on **TOGGLE HOLDS** (below).

### TOGGLE HOLDS

- **Where:** [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#controls), Controls tab. Off by default.
- **What it does:** one press starts a crank; it keeps turning on its own.
- **To stop:** press interact again, walk away or pause. It also stops when the crank is done.
- **Works on** keyboard, controller and touch, because all three press the same interact action.
- **Prompts change:** they drop the word **HOLD** (for example **E: crank the valve**).
- **It is saved** with your other preferences. **RESTORE DEFAULT CONTROLS** does not change it.

<Details>
<Summary>Crank timing</Summary>

- **VALVE:** each 0.4 seconds held is one turn. Three turns charge the door.
- **MANUAL BYPASS:** each quarter second held is one of eight notches, two seconds in all.

</Details>

## Keyboard and mouse

- **Answers appear as buttons** on screen when a choice is waiting, so you can click them.
- **Title and pause menus** take the mouse as well as the keyboard.

## Controller

- **The selected menu item** shows a cyan outline inside its border.
- **Closing Settings** from the title returns the selection to **SETTINGS**.

## Touch (iOS and Android builds)

- **The arrow buttons hide** during the opening, the closing scene and while a question waits.
- **PAUSE** opens Resume, Settings and Return to Title (Skip Opening during the opening).
- **Leaving the app** during play pauses the game.
- **Android Back** steps back one menu, and pauses play. It closes the game only from the title screen.
- **Touch menus show no outline.** The first arrow key or controller press selects an item without using it.
- **The USE button changes its name:** **CRANK** at a crank, **DROP** at the tank release, **USE** elsewhere.
- **Answers** replace the arrows and **USE** at the bottom, side by side, within thumb reach.

<Details>
<Summary>Where things sit on a touch screen</Summary>

- The hint line sits between the arrows and **USE**, so neither covers it.
- With **TEXT SIZE** above 100%, the dialogue box moves into that space too, with the hint line just above it.
- Touch answers show their words only, without the 1 and 2 of the keyboard.
- The camera sits a little lower on touch, so feet and machine bases stay above the buttons. It stays there while answers show.

</Details>
