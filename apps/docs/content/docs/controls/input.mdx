title: Input
description: Default keyboard, controller, and touch inputs for the current source build.
---

You control the engineer. WOLF is an independent companion. In-game prompts show the keyboard or controller binding you have set; these are the defaults.

Every room uses the same small set of inputs: move, interact, the two answers and pause. The Service Junction adds no new buttons; its valve is cranked by **holding** interact, and its prompt says **HOLD** when that applies. Each 0.4 seconds held is one turn, and turns already made stay done if you let go or pause.

| Action | Keyboard | Controller | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or ← / → | Left stick or D-pad left / right | Hold ◀ / ▶ |
| Interact; continue the opening or closing beat | E | A | USE (labelled CONTINUE during the opening and closing beat) |
| Crank the Service Junction valve | Hold E | Hold A | Hold USE (labelled CRANK there) |
| Answer 1 / Answer 2 | 1 / 2, or click the response | X / Y | Tap the response |
| Pause or resume | Esc | Start | PAUSE button; tap RESUME to continue |
| Back in menus | Esc | B | Android Back |
| Cancel a rebind | Esc or CANCEL REBIND | — | Android Back |
| Cycle the provisional engineer name | I (fixed; not rebindable) | — | — |

Move, Interact, both answers, and Pause can be rebound in [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/). Controller labels are generic; your controller may name the same buttons differently.

## Notes by device

**Keyboard and mouse:** response buttons also appear on screen when a choice is waiting, so you can click them. The title and pause menus take the mouse as well as the keyboard.

**Controller:** the selected menu item shows a cyan outline inside its border. Closing Settings from the title returns the selection to **SETTINGS**.

**Touch:** on iOS and Android builds the direction buttons hide during the opening, the closing beat, and while a choice is waiting. **PAUSE** opens Resume, Settings, and Return to Title (Skip Opening during the opening). On Android, the system Back gesture backs out of menus one step at a time and pauses play; it only closes the game from the title screen. Leaving the app during play pauses the game. Menus opened by touch show no selection outline; the first arrow key or controller press selects a menu item without activating it. At the Service Junction's arm panel **USE** reads **DROP**. While the arrows and **USE** are on screen the camera sits a little lower, so the characters' feet and the machines' bases stay above the buttons; it rises back while answer buttons are shown.

**Status:** controller hardware and physical touch devices have not been playtested; one iOS simulator launch was checked. There is no public mobile download yet. For checkpoint behaviour, see [Saving](/docs/controls/saves/).
