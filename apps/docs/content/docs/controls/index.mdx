**What this page is for:** pick the page that answers your question.

- **You play the engineer.** WOLF follows and acts on his own.
- **Default keys:** **A / D** or arrows to move, **E** to interact, **1 / 2** to answer, **Esc** to pause.
- **Prompts follow your bindings** if you change them in Settings.
