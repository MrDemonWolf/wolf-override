This quick tutorial covers the basics of your first session without giving away puzzle solutions. You play the engineer; WOLF is an independent companion who moves and acts on his own.

## 1. Start a new run

Choose **New Game** from the title screen. The opening scene introduces the situation before you enter the facility. Press **E** (or your mapped interact button) to advance each beat. Press **Esc** to pause; choose **Skip Opening** from Pause to go straight to play.

The title screen also has **CREDITS** and **CHANGELOG**, which open inside the game. The changelog screen lists the same dated updates as [this website's changelog](/docs/changelog/); scroll it with the mouse wheel, by dragging the text or with up/down, and use **OPEN FULL CHANGELOG ONLINE** for the website version. **BACK TO TITLE**, Esc, controller B or Android Back return to the title.

## 2. Move and look around

Use **A / D** or **← / →** to move left and right. On a controller, use the left stick or D-pad. On a touch build, hold the on-screen direction buttons.

Watch for labeled stations and the nearby prompt. The prompt tells you when you are close enough to interact and which button your current input setup uses.

At the start, a small tutorial box near the top of the screen shows how to move and suggests reading the nearby display. That display is optional. The objective card points you to your first real goal; once you head that way, the tutorial box follows it and disappears when you arrive or interact with anything.

## 3. Interact with the world

Press the prompted interact button (**E** by default; **USE** on touch) when you are near a station. Interacting can reveal information, advance dialogue, or operate something. Some stations are optional; follow the current objective card and station labels to make progress.

The game may briefly show an objective card when your goal changes. If you are unsure what to do, check the station prompt before trying another control.

## 4. Choose how to respond

When numbered responses appear, choose **1** or **2**, click a response, or use the displayed controller/touch option. Take your time: dialogue and choices wait for your input.

WOLF has his own boundaries. A choice can change what he says or does, but the current chapter keeps essential progress available to the engineer too. You always control the engineer, never WOLF.

## 5. Pause and save

Press **Esc** during play to pause. Reach the green **SAFE POINT** and interact with it to save a checkpoint. From the title screen, **Continue** resumes a valid checkpoint. There is one checkpoint file in the current build.

For the complete input list, controller and touch notes, open the [input guide](/docs/controls/input/). For exactly how Continue, New Game, and checkpoint recovery work, see [saving and resuming](/docs/controls/saves/).

**Need help?** Check [settings](/docs/controls/settings/) or [troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/). If you want optional puzzle solutions, the [chapter walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/) is marked for spoilers.
