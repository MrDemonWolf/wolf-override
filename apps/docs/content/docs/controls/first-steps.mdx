**What this page is for:** the start of your first run, as numbered steps. No puzzle solutions.

- **You play the engineer.** WOLF is a companion who moves and acts on his own. You never control him.
- **Keys below are the defaults.** [Controls](/docs/controls/input/) has the controller and touch versions.
- **New words?** The [FAQ glossary](/docs/faq/#glossary) explains them.

## Start a new run

1. On the title screen, choose **New Game**.
2. The opening scene plays. Press **E** (or tap **CONTINUE** on touch) to show the next line.
3. Want to skip it? Press **Esc** (or **PAUSE** on touch) and choose **Skip Opening**.

### Also on the title screen

- **CREDITS** and **CHANGELOG** open inside the game.
- **The version number** sits under **CHANGELOG**.
- **The changelog screen** shows the short lines of the [website changelog](/docs/changelog/). **OPEN FULL CHANGELOG ONLINE** opens the website version.
- **Scroll the changelog** with the mouse wheel, by dragging the text, or with up / down.
- **Leave it** with **BACK TO TITLE**, **Esc**, controller **B** or Android Back.

## Move and look around

1. Move with **A / D** or **← / →** (left stick or D-pad on a controller; hold the arrow buttons on touch).
2. Take a first step. The engineer's name card fades.
3. Read the small prompt near the top. It tells you what to try next.
4. Head right. The tutorial box points you to the first goal.

- **WOLF follows on his own.** He keeps about a body length away, on the side you are not facing.
- **The objective card** shows briefly when your goal changes.
- **Reading the nearby display** at the start is optional.

<Details>
<Summary>More about WOLF and the first prompts</Summary>

- When you turn around, WOLF crosses behind you to your other side.
- Next to a wall or a shut door, he stays on the open side.
- If you stand still, the objective card follows the name card and points to your first real goal.
- The tutorial box disappears when you arrive or interact with anything. While it shows, it is the only prompt on screen.

</Details>

## Use things

1. Walk up to a labelled station. A prompt appears when you are close enough.
2. Press **E** (**USE** on touch) to interact.
3. Follow the objective card and the station names to make progress.

- **Some stations are optional.** They show extra information.
- **In the Service Junction, a press that can't work yet still answers.** The machine's lamp flickers, a dull clunk plays and a line says why.
- **Press again** and the dialogue box blinks as the line comes back.
- **Stuck for a while?** WOLF looks at what matters. Later, the engineer says more.
- **Some machines need a hold.** When the prompt says **HOLD**, keep the button down. See [Hold to crank](/docs/controls/input/#hold-to-crank).

## Answer a question

1. Two numbered answers appear.
2. Press **1** or **2**, click an answer, or use the controller button shown.
3. On touch, tap one of the two answer buttons at the bottom of the screen.

- **There is no timer.** A question stays up until you answer.
- **Answers change what WOLF says or does.** They never block the chapter. The engineer always has a way forward.

## Read the dialogue

- **Lines appear in a box** at the bottom of the screen, one speaker at a time. When two people speak in one beat, each gets their own box.
- **The speaker's name** sits beside the words. Narration has no name.
- **Long lines split** into short boxes that follow one another. No word changes.
- **You can keep walking** while people talk. Each box stays up for a reading time set by its length.
- **To go faster**, press interact in the opening, the closing scene, or at a station whose hint says **continue**.
- **Text too small?** Raise **TEXT SIZE** in [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#display).

<Details>
<Summary>How long each box stays up</Summary>

- A box holds at most two short rows.
- Its reading time is about a third of a second per word or a twentieth of a second per character, whichever is longer, plus a moment.
- The last box stays until you move on.

</Details>

## If you get knocked down

Later in the chapter, steam, blasts and machines can knock the engineer down.

1. The screen flashes red and the engineer falls.
2. Read the line. It names what hit you and how to avoid it.
3. Press **E** when the hint shows **RETRY** (**TAP RETRY** on touch).

- **You lose only the step you were on.** Your earlier choices are kept.
- **There is no penalty** for being knocked down or for taking the careful route.
- **Details:** [Knockdowns](/docs/controls/saves/#knockdowns) on the Saving page.

## Pause and save

1. Press **Esc** to pause.
2. Reach the green **SAFE POINT** and interact with it. That is your first save.
3. After that, the game also saves on its own at key moments.
4. Next time, choose **Continue** on the title screen.

- **How saving works:** [Saving](/docs/controls/saves/).
- **Something wrong?** [Troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/).
- **Stuck on a puzzle?** The [walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/) has spoilers.
