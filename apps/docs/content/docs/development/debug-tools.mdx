**What this page is for:** see what the game is doing, jump anywhere in the slice, and save a record of a problem.

- **For players:** to report a bug with a capture, see [Support and bug reports](/docs/support/).
- **Debug build** means the editor, a source run with the standard Godot binary, or a [test build](/docs/test-builds/). The tools are always available there.
- **Any other build** keeps them hidden until you enter a code on the title screen.

## Unlock the tools

| Input | Code |
| --- | --- |
| Keyboard | Type `WOLFDEN`, in capitals or not. Shift, Caps Lock and the other modifier keys do not break it; any other key does. |
| Controller | On the D-pad: Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, then B (the east face button), then A (the south face button). The final A does not press the focused menu button. |
| Touch | Tap the WOLF OVERRIDE logo mark at the top right seven times, each tap within a second of the last. |

- **A short "Debug tools unlocked" note** confirms it.
- **The unlock is saved** with your settings, so the tools stay on in later launches.
- **Locking** them again also forgets the saved unlock.

## Keys and buttons

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **F3** | Cycles the overlay: off, performance, state, state and zones. |
| **F5**, **F6**, **F7** | On the zones page: zones, hitboxes and render boxes on or off. The legend's buttons at the top of the screen do the same with a tap or click. |
| **LB**, **RB**, **View** (Select / Back) | The same on a controller, on the zones page. A button you bound to a game action in Settings is left alone. The legend's caption names the keys, buttons or taps for the device in use. |
| **F12** | Saves a bug capture. |
| `` ` `` (backquote), both sticks pressed (L3 + R3), or the **DEBUG** button | Opens or closes the debug menu. |
| **.** (period), **RB**, or **STEP** | While frame stepping: lets one frame of play through. |
| **Esc**, **B**, or **RUN** | While frame stepping: plays on at normal speed. |

- **DEBUG button:** in touch play it sits at the top right, a finger's gap left of **PAUSE**, clear of the characters, **USE** and the arrows.
- **It hides** while Pause, Settings, the END OF SLICE card, Credits or Changelog is open, or a controller is in use.

## The overlay

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Performance | Frames per second, frame and process time, memory, and node, object and draw-call counts |
| State | Where everyone is, what they are doing, the run's choices and the save |
| State and zones | The state card, plus a drawing of the game's rule shapes over the scene |

<Details>
<Summary>Performance page detail</Summary>

- Godot reports the game's own (static) memory only in debug builds.
- So in an unlocked release build that figure reads **n/a**, and the page shows video memory instead.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>State page detail</Summary>

- The room, the current beat and objective.
- The engineer's position, facing and what they are doing.
- WOLF's position, where he is heading and why: following, scouting, holding a spot, drawing the sentry or refusing.
- In the Service Junction: the sentry's state, position, facing, sight range and who it is fixed on; the pressure line's state, gauge, turns and fuse; the tank arm; the exit bolt; which hazards are armed.
- The run's relay, mirror and lane choices and WOLF's memory.
- The save format version, whether a checkpoint is on disk and where it stands.
- Whether cheats were used.
- It keeps updating while the game is paused, and reads the save file at most once a second.

</Details>

### Zones page

The drawing follows the camera and is worked out every frame from the same shapes and constants the game's rules use. It has three colour-coded layers, named in a small legend at the top:

| Layer | Colour | What it draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zones | Cyan | Interact ranges, where people step and stand, WOLF's follow distance, the sentry's lane and the limits of the room |
| Hitboxes | Orange, red when in force | Every collision shape and every range that hurts or hits |
| Render boxes | Yellow-green | The on-screen box of every sprite, prop, label, button and panel, with its name, z-index and canvas layer |

<Details>
<Summary>Zones in detail</Summary>

- Drawn as bars in lanes under the floor line, with end ticks.
- Each station's interact range, drawn faint while it does not answer (such as the **TANK RELEASE** before the door is down).
- The spots the engineer steps to, and WOLF's spots (with his current target and why, in white).
- His follow distance around the engineer and the machines he rests clear of.
- The sentry's lane in the Service Junction: the alcove, the patrol, its sight as an arrow from where it faces, and where it watches from.
- Dashed lines for limits: walls, a shut door's face, how far WOLF follows, the lane entry, the sentry's chase limit and where WOLF asks about the lane.
- The camera's centre limits and its current position sit on the bottom edge of the screen.
- A range wholly off screen is listed in captures but not drawn.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Hitboxes in detail</Summary>

- Every collision shape from its real shape: both characters, the floor, the corridor seal and the junction door while they block, the sentry's body, and the relief vent and coolant cloud hurtboxes.
- The rule ranges that hurt or hit: the vent and cloud by the engineer's position, the door blast (with the room above it shaded), the sentry's contact, and the tank's hit range and footprint.
- Anything in force right now (an armed hurtbox, a lit fuse, a falling tank) turns red.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Render boxes in detail</Summary>

- The on-screen rectangle of every sprite, prop, plate layer, label, button and panel.
- Also props the game draws in code: the sentry's sight beam and lens, the bypass's crank lights and leader, room lights and beacons.
- Each is named with its node name, z-index and canvas layer, for example `BodySprite z2` or `PauseButton z0 L2`, so overlaps and anything drawn inside something else stand out.
- Boxes hidden behind the title or the end card, faded out or off screen are listed in captures but not drawn.
- Boxes for props drawn in code come from what the renderer recorded, which Godot reports only in editor builds. In an exported build only props that declare their area (the sentry's beam) get one, and captures say which source each box used.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>What the drawing never covers</Summary>

- The touch arrows, **USE**, the answer buttons, **PAUSE**, and the text of the hint, the dialogue, the tutorial prompt and the top card: lines, bars and fills are cut around them.
- Every name is placed where it overlaps none of them, no character and no other name. Smaller boxes are named first; a name moved away from its box is joined to it by a short line.
- A name with no room left is not drawn. Captures still list it, with `label_hidden`, and list the protected areas under `keep_clear`.
- The drawing steps aside with the overlay for Pause, Settings and the game's cards, and costs nothing while another page is up.

</Details>

### When the overlay steps aside

- **The game's own screens come first.** The overlay hides while Pause, Settings, the END OF SLICE card, or the title's Credits or Changelog is open.
- **Touch layout:** it also hides while a question's answer buttons are up, and comes back when they close.
- **Keyboard and controller layout:** it stays up during a question, where the answers are clear of it.
- **Tutorial prompt:** while the corridor's prompt is up, the state and zones pages narrow to end left of it, so its control instructions stay readable. They widen again once it has gone.

## The debug menu

- **It holds the game still** while open, as Pause does, and sits right of the overlay so the state page stays readable.
- **Keyboard or controller:** focus starts on the page's tab. Move along the tabs (or press **LB** / **RB**), move down into the page and confirm a button.
- **Touch:** tap a tab or a button.
- **Close it** with **Esc**, **B**, Android Back, **CLOSE**, or a tap outside the card. It does not open while the game's own Pause or Settings is up.
- **Every action** notes what it did, or why it cannot, under the page.

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **JUMP** | Puts you straight at a point of the slice, built from the **CHOICES** presets and loaded as Continue does |
| **CHOICES** | The earlier choices the next jump uses. Not a cheat |
| **CHEATS** | Invincible, freeze the sentry, instant crank, frame step and slow motion |
| **FORCE** | Knockdowns, the door fuse and the tank drop, on demand |
| **SAVE** | See, write, reload or clear the checkpoint |
| **VIEW** | Overlay page, bug capture and effect switches; lock the tools |

<Details>
<Summary>JUMP: every point and what it does to your save</Summary>

- **Points:** the opening, the start of the corridor, WOLF's relay question, the corridor's safe point, the start of Records Access, Records Access with both copies made, the start of the Service Junction, the lane after the door blast (with WOLF already asked and answered from the **CHOICES** page), the sentry down, or the **END OF SLICE** card.
- **How:** each jump builds the run through the game's own save steps from the **CHOICES** presets and loads it as Continue does.
- **Your save:** a jump to a point the game autosaves at writes that checkpoint, as play does, replacing the save on disk. The opening, corridor start and relay question leave the save alone.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>CHOICES: the presets</Summary>

- **The relay:** WOLF rushed and the engineer cranked the bypass; WOLF told and taking the contact; or WOLF told and the engineer cranking anyway.
- **The mirror:** WOLF's readout or the manual port.
- **The sentry:** WOLF draws it, the engineer times it alone, or WOLF refuses.
- **Same rule as play:** asking WOLF to draw the sentry after a rushed relay turns into a refusal; the presets change together and say so.
- **Not a cheat:** choosing presets does not mark the run.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>CHEATS: each one</Summary>

- **INVINCIBLE:** no knockdown lands; the door still blows as if the engineer were clear.
- **FREEZE SENTRY:** the sentry stops moving and changing state. Touching one that was chasing still knocks you down unless invincible.
- **INSTANT CRANK:** holding interact at the junction valve or the corridor's **MANUAL BYPASS** finishes it at once, through the game's own crank.
- **FRAME STEP:** the game holds still and a small bar near the top right lets one frame through per **STEP** (or **.** / **RB**), and plays on with **RUN** (or **Esc** / **B**). A jump or **RELOAD CHECKPOINT** while stepping keeps the game held.
- **SPEED:** 1x, 0.5x or 0.25x. The game's short impact pauses still dip it further for their moment.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>FORCE: knockdowns and triggers</Summary>

- **Knockdowns** (Service Junction only): the relief vent's, the door blast's, the sentry's or the coolant cloud's own card, with the same short pause, red flash and camera shake as in play, even while invincible. The beat's checkpoint comes back as usual.
- **LIGHT THE DOOR FUSE:** arms and cranks the pressure line, so the door goes about two and a half seconds after play resumes. Where everyone stands then decides the outcome.
- **DROP THE TANK:** lets the tank go from wherever you are. It lands on the sentry only if the sentry is over the floor brackets.
- **Greyed out** buttons cannot work now; the reason is shown below.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>SAVE and VIEW</Summary>

- **SAVE** shows the checkpoint on disk.
- **SAVE NOW** writes the current beat through the game's own autosave, only once the first safe point is reached.
- **RELOAD CHECKPOINT** goes back to it. **CLEAR SAVE** deletes it after a second press.
- **OVERLAY** steps the overlay's page (for phones, which have no F3).
- **BUG CAPTURE** closes the menu and saves a capture.
- **VISUAL EFFECTS**, **CAMERA SHAKE** and **SCREEN FLASH** turn those off for this session. They are not cheats and are not saved.
- **LOCK DEBUG TOOLS** hides the tools and forgets the unlock.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Where the result note shows</Summary>

- Actions that close the menu so their result plays (jumps, forced knockdowns, the fuse, the tank and reloading) leave their note for a few seconds at the top of the screen, between the top card and **PAUSE**, over nothing but the ceiling.
- On the zones page, whose legend has that spot, the note sits just below the answer row or under **PAUSE**, whichever covers neither character nor the Service Junction's tank.
- Opening the menu again clears it.

</Details>

### Cheats are marked

- **CHEATS USED:** jumps, cheats, forced knockdowns and the two triggers mark the overlay and every dump.
- **State page:** lists the cheats in force, for example `invincible · 0.5x`.
- **Dumps and captures** list every cheat, preset and switch under `debug.cheats`.
- **Locking the tools,** or a build where they become unavailable, turns every cheat off, puts the speed and effects back and lets the game run.
- **No cost when idle:** while the tools are hidden, or available with the overlay off, the menu closed and no slow motion or instant crank on, they do no work each frame and draw nothing.

## Bug captures

1. Press **F12** (or **BUG CAPTURE** on the **VIEW** page).
2. The game saves a screenshot and a JSON file to `user://debug-captures/`, Godot's user data folder for the game, named by date and time.
3. It shows the path. Attach both files to your [bug report](/docs/support/).

- **Headless runs** write the JSON only.
- **The JSON** holds the overlay's data, plus positions and flags.
- **Where `user://` is** on your system: [Saving and retrying](/docs/controls/saves/).

<Details>
<Summary>The JSON's geometry</Summary>

- `geometry` lists every zone, hitbox and render box as numbers, whether or not they were drawn.
- Each has its node path, `world_rect` and `screen_rect` as `[x, y, width, height]`, z-index, canvas layer, visibility, alpha and draw order.
- Screen rects are in the 960×540 canvas, with the scale to window pixels beside them.
- The camera is included.

</Details>

## For capture scripts

- **`debug_tools.dump()`** on the game's main node logs the same data beside your own screenshots.
- **`await debug_tools.capture_with_boxes()`** saves a capture with every box drawn into the screenshot, whatever page is up.
- **The tools' own note** (an earlier capture's path, a jump's result) is hidden first, so it is never in the picture.
- **No frame drawn?** If the window draws no frame within ten frames (hidden or covered), that call still saves the JSON, skips the out-of-date screenshot, reports `with_boxes: false` and puts the page back.
- **Rendered captures** on a developer machine go through the [render container](/docs/development/setup/#rendered-captures), never a game window.

## Where the code lives

- **Folder:** `apps/game/scripts/debug/`.
- **One door into the game:** the tools reach the game only through the main script's typed fields and its public `debug_*` methods (`debug_snapshot`, `debug_new_game`, `debug_load_checkpoint`, `debug_knock_down` and the like), kept together at the end of `main.gd`.
- **Why:** never its private ones, so renaming or moving one of those fails when the scripts are parsed, not when a menu button is pressed.
- **Map of the rest:** [Architecture](/docs/development/architecture/).
