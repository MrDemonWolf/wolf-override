**What this page is for:** the steps a picture goes through before it is in the game.

- **All current game art is provisional.** Most of it is AI-generated, then reviewed by a person.
- **Who made what, and with which tool:** [Credits: Provisional art](/docs/credits/#provisional-art).
- **Rights:** [Legal: Artwork and other assets](/docs/legal/#artwork-and-other-assets). The art is not covered by the code's GPL licence.

## Words used here

- **Whole piece:** one complete image of one thing: a character in one pose, a room layer, a prop. Never a body part or a kit of parts.
- **Plate:** one painted layer of a room: far, middle or near.
- **Atlas:** one image holding several pieces, cut apart by the game.
- **Asset register:** [`apps/game/assets/manifest.json`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/blob/main/apps/game/assets/manifest.json), one entry per file.
- **C2PA content credentials:** metadata inside an image that records it was made with an AI tool. Which images keep them: [Credits](/docs/credits/#tools-used-in-the-current-source-build).

## The steps

1. **Design the exact shape first.** Scripts draw each piece's shape from real measurements (real doors, gearboxes, cabinets) or from the earlier art as a guide.
2. **Paint inside the shape.** The image tool paints material and light only inside those shapes. It does not decide the shape.
3. **Clean up.** Scripts cut out each piece and clean up the result. Later fixes are made without new paint where possible.
4. **Check for AI tells.** Look for things that read as generated: odd lettering, extra parts, objects that hover, doors that make no sense.
5. **Try one piece in the game first,** then the rest of the set.
6. **Look at rendered stills.** Captures come from the [render container](/docs/development/setup/#rendered-captures), before and after.
7. **Get approval.** Nathanial Henniges reviews and approves each set.
8. **Register it.** Add the asset register entry in the same pull request.
9. **Replace, don't pile up.** New art fully replaces old: delete the old files, imports, register entries and references.

## How a scene is built

- **Rooms** are three plates (far, middle, near) that move at different speeds as the camera follows.
- **Each character, prop and station** is its own whole piece, stacked in code.
- **Light is drawn by the game,** not painted in: lamp glows, flicker, beacons, haze, dust, floor reflections, and WOLF's eye and seam lights.
- **Doors** are atlas pieces, some drawn as meshes so they can swing or slide.
- **Lettering** (signs, stencils) is typeset in Roboto, never painted by the image tool.
- **Code:** `room_depth.gd` builds the rooms; the map of the rest is on [Architecture](/docs/development/architecture/).

## Register every file

Every image, sound, font or other media file needs an entry before it is added to the game or the site. Each entry records at least:

| Field | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| `asset_id` | A stable name |
| `path` | Where the file is |
| `source_or_tool` | Who or what made it |
| `terms_reference` | The terms it was made under |
| `rights_review_status` | Where its rights review stands |
| `modifications` | What the project changed |
| `implementation_status` | Whether and where it is used |

- **Existing entries also keep a `source_reference`** for the prompt, review sheet or download. Follow their example.
- **AI-generated art needs a rights note:** tool and date, references used, that a person reviewed and approved it, and that its commercial rights review is open. Details: [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#assets-and-provenance).

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not add">
  Music, recorded audio or third-party art without a licence that allows redistribution. Credits for people, music or tools that were not involved.
</Callout>

## Where the working files go

- **Not in the game:** design notes, review sheets and raw generated images are not exported.
- **Notes** now live in the project's Notion, indexed in [`docs/NOTES.md`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/blob/main/docs/NOTES.md).
- **Older art records** (October 12 to 14, 2026) stay in the repository's history; the links are on [Credits](/docs/credits/#provisional-art).
