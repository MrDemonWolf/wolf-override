WOLF//OVERRIDE is an early source prototype. It contains two connected playable rooms, not a complete investigation or campaign. There is no public download, release date, or announced price.

## In the source build

- The engineer is the sole playable character. WOLF accompanies them independently; both have provisional static art.
- The corridor has a power and door puzzle with a cooperative route and a manual route if WOLF refuses.
- One authored disagreement is remembered, with a later callback and safe checkpoint.
- The opening, purge display, station feedback, and objective cards guide the first steps.
- Records Access lets the engineer copy a purge-order trace, then preserve a timestamp through WOLF's independent readout or a manual maintenance port.
- New Game and versioned save/load cover both rooms, positions, and chapter progress.
- Controller mappings, mobile on-screen controls, and saved Display, Audio, and Controls preferences are present. [Controls and settings](/docs/controls/) explains them.

## Still to come

Final character art and animation, audio, the wider story, public export packages, complete touch/controller playtests, and physical-device testing remain future work. The corridor key art on the landing page is provisional and is not a gameplay capture. Its source and rights-review status are in the [asset register](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/blob/main/apps/game/assets/manifest.json).

## Direct macOS updater

**Planned, not integrated or enabled.** The game has no Sparkle framework, updater object, update feed, or automatic network check. Sparkle is a candidate for direct macOS downloads, but a Godot app needs a tested native integration and repeatable export packaging. Store builds should use their store channels.

No keys, feed, native dependency, or signing changes have been created. Packaging, signing, update verification, and save compatibility must be checked before a release could enable it. References: [Sparkle setup](https://sparkle-project.org/documentation/) and [programmatic setup](https://sparkle-project.org/documentation/programmatic-setup/).

See [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/) for what checks have and have not established. The [changelog](/docs/changelog/) records source changes by date.
