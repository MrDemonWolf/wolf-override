**What this page is for:** what is built, what is unproven, and what comes next.

- **An early source prototype:** three connected puzzle rooms and a maintenance annex, not a complete campaign.
- **No public download, release date or announced price.** Version history: [Changelog](/docs/changelog/).

## Built

### Story and play

- **The engineer is the sole playable character.** WOLF accompanies them independently.
- **The corridor** has a power and door puzzle, with a cooperative route and a manual route if WOLF refuses.
- **One authored disagreement is remembered,** with a later callback and a safe checkpoint.
- **First steps are guided** by the opening, the purge display, station feedback and objective cards.
- **Records Access:** copy a purge-order trace, then preserve a timestamp through WOLF's independent readout or a manual maintenance port.
- **The Service Junction** turns the building into the engineer's only tool. Spoiler-light summary below.
- **Maintenance annex:** three engineer-operated record checks extend the route beyond the Service Junction, using existing room art as provisional staging.
- **CHAPTER 1 COMPLETE:** the annex's final exit leads to a recap card. On a finished save the title offers **PLAY AGAIN**.

<Details>
<Summary>The Service Junction in one list (light spoilers)</Summary>

- It lies past the Records exit.
- Overload a breaker, crank a valve with a held interact, and blow a sealed door on a fuse of about two and a half seconds that ticks as it burns.
- Drop a hanging coolant tank on a patrolling sentry machine. WOLF may choose to draw it onto the mark, or the engineer times the drop alone.
- Pop the exit bolt.
- Knockdowns (the relief vent, the blast, the sentry, the coolant cloud) return to the room's last checkpoint.
- The exit continues into the maintenance annex; follow its station prompts to finish the chapter.

</Details>

### Look and sound

- **Rooms** are built from provisional AI-assisted painted layers (far, middle and near) that move at different speeds as the camera follows.
- **Each station is its own machine,** the Service Junction's sentry, arm and tank included.
- **Room light:** lamp glows that flicker at random, lockdown beacons that settle once the room's route is cleared, haze, drifting dust and soft floor reflections. Only the room in play animates its light and dust.
- **Camera:** sits close (1.35x) and follows the engineer.
- **Characters:** provisional art, redrawn in October 2026 with AI assistance and approved by Nathanial Henniges.
- **Big moments** answer with short sounds (most generated by the game itself, a few from processed CC0 recordings), a brief camera shake, sparks or dust, controller rumble or phone vibration and, for the biggest, a screen flash.

### Menus, saves and settings

- **New Game and versioned save/load** cover the route, positions and chapter progress, including the annex's entry and completed record checks.
- **Settings:** saved Display, Audio and Controls preferences, including Reduced Motion and Text Size. See [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/) and [Accessibility](/docs/accessibility/).
- **Input:** controller mappings and mobile on-screen controls. See [Controls](/docs/controls/input/).
- **In-game changelog:** the title screen's **CHANGELOG** opens these development updates, generated from the website [changelog](/docs/changelog/). The title shows the version number.
- **Debug tools** for testers: overlay, debug menu and F12 bug captures. See [Debug tools](/docs/development/debug-tools/).

### Builds and languages

- **Test builds:** unsigned macOS and Windows debug builds from each change. See [Install a test build](/docs/test-builds/).
- **Languages:** English, plus German, Spanish (Latin America and Spain), French, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) for the menus, the HUD and the story, machine-translated and not yet reviewed. See [Localization](/docs/development/localization/).

## Not yet played by hand

  The established rooms have headless tests and rendered stills. The maintenance annex is a new implementation candidate with final validation pending. No complete route has been played by hand.
