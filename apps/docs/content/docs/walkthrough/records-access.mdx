**Puzzle solutions for the second source room follow.** The [maintenance corridor](/docs/walkthrough/corridor/) comes first.

Entering the room saves your progress, and WOLF heads toward the mirror independently while you keep control of the engineer. Each station below saves again when it succeeds.

## 1. Copy the purge trace

Interact with the **PURGE QUEUE** on the left to copy its trace.

## 2. Preserve a timestamp

Interact with the **MIRROR PORT** in the middle. Choose **1** when WOLF is ready to use his readout, or **2** for the manual maintenance port. If he is still checking the drive, wait for him or use the manual option.

## 3. Open the exit

Move to the **EXIT** on the right and interact. The shutter opens when the first copy is secured. Press **E** (**CONTINUE** on touch) to end the closing beat and return to control, then interact at the open exit again to follow the service line into the [Service Junction](/docs/walkthrough/service-junction/).

The preserved traces are a first lead. A full investigation is still future work. **Continue** restores saved chapter progress and tells you which copy is next or that the exit is open.

## After the slice

You can revisit the room, return to the title through Pause, or start another run to try the other response. **New Game** clears the current timeline; it retains the previous checkpoint until the new run saves over it.

If you are stuck, [Troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/) has recovery tips. **Status:** both mirror routes pass headless checks, but the room has not been played through by hand; see [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/).
