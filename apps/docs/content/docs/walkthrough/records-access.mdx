**What this page is for:** how to get through Records Access, step by step.

**Route:** Purge queue → Mirror port → Exit.

- **Entering the room saves.** Each station saves again when it works.
- **WOLF heads to the mirror** on his own. You keep control of the engineer.

## 1. Copy the purge trace

**Do this:**

1. Go to the **PURGE QUEUE** on the left.
2. Press interact. A chirp confirms the copy.

## 2. Preserve a timestamp

**Do this:**

1. Go to the **MIRROR PORT** in the middle.
2. Press interact and choose an answer:
   - **1:** WOLF uses his own readout, when he is ready.
   - **2:** you use the manual maintenance port.
3. A chirp confirms the copy.

- **WOLF still checking the drive?** Wait for him, or choose the manual port.
- **He stands just right of the port,** leaving its left side for you.

## 3. Open the exit

**Do this:**

1. Go to the **EXIT** on the right, beside the dark security container.
2. The hint reads **E: secure the first copy in the container.** Press interact.
3. The door unlocks and swings open. The closing lines play while you move.
4. When the lines are done, press interact at the open door to leave.

- **Want the lines faster?** Press interact at the exit (the hint says **continue**).
- **Next room:** [Service Junction](/docs/walkthrough/service-junction/).

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- You come in through a steel swing door. Its closer swings it shut behind you, and its lamp turns red.
- The exit is a steel door in a concrete wall across the walkway.
- When you secure the copy, the bolt lets go, the lamp turns amber and the door swings open on its hinges. Its lamp turns green once it is open.
- If you interact elsewhere during the closing lines, that answer shows first. The closing lines come back after it, where they left off.
- The engineer walks out into the service line, and WOLF follows.

</Details>

## After the room

- **The copies are a first lead.** A full investigation is future work.
- **Continue** tells you which copy is next, or that the exit is open.
- **Try the other answer:** start a **New Game**. The old checkpoint stays until the new run saves over it.
