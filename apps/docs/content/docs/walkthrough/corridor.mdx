## 1. Read the corridor

After the opening, move the engineer with **A/D** or the arrow keys. An interaction prompt appears near a station. At the far-left red **PURGE DISPLAY**, **E** reads optional context; this is not needed to open the door.

These are default keys. If you changed bindings, use the inputs shown by the game. See the [input guide](/docs/controls/input/).

## 2. Arm the breaker

Move right to the amber **BREAKER**. Interact to hear WOLF's concern about coolant risk. Choose answer **1** or **2**, then interact again to arm power.

The answer changes his response at the relay and is remembered later. Repeated station use does not add duplicate consequences.

## 3. Open the sealed door

Continue right to the blue **COOLANT RELAY**.

### WOLF-assisted route

If you chose **1**, telling WOLF about the risk, stand toward the relay's right side and interact. He needs room to reach his contact. If he asks for space, move a few steps right while the relay prompt stays visible, then interact again.

### Engineer-only route

If you chose **2**, pressing WOLF to act, interact to hear his refusal. Interact again to perform the engineer's manual bypass.

Both routes open the door. You never switch to controlling WOLF.

## 4. Use the safe point

Walk through the door to the green **SAFE POINT** and interact. WOLF recalls the answer from this run. The checkpoint saves positions, door state, display name, and memory.

Interact again to enter Records Access. A valid checkpoint enables **Continue** on the title screen. **L/F9** restores it during play. For the next room, open the [Records Access guide](/docs/walkthrough/records-access/).
