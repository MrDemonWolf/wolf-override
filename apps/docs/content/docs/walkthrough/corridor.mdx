## 1. Read the corridor

**Do this:**

1. Move with **A / D** or the arrow keys. Your first step fades the name card.
2. Optional: press **E** at the red purge display on the far left to read it.
3. Head right toward the breaker.

- **The opening lines** play as short boxes while you walk.
- **You can skip the display.** WOLF sums it up when you walk past.
- **Keys are defaults.** If you rebound them, use what the game shows. See [Controls](/docs/controls/input/).

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- A card names the engineer, and a prompt shows how to move. You can move straight away.
- The opening lines say why the engineer trusts WOLF, then WOLF and the engineer talk.
- If you reach the breaker before the lines are done, WOLF sums up the display there. The hint offers **continue**, and interact goes on through his lines to his question.
- If you stop with the engineer over a machine, they sidestep out beside it. Any move takes over at once.

</Details>

## 2. Arm the breaker

**Do this:**

1. Go right to the amber **BREAKER**.
2. Press interact. WOLF asks about the relay's **COOLANT FAULT** tag.
3. Choose answer **1** or **2**.
4. Press interact again to arm the power.

- **Your answer changes** how WOLF acts at the relay. He remembers it later.
- **Using the station again** adds no extra consequences.

## 3. Open the sealed door

Go right to the blue **COOLANT RELAY**. The handwheel beside the door is the **MANUAL BYPASS**. Your answer decides which route opens.

| Your answer | What happens | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** (warn WOLF about the risk) | WOLF takes the relay's live contact himself | Press interact at the relay |
| **2** (tell him to go now) | WOLF refuses | Crank the **MANUAL BYPASS** |

### Route 1: WOLF helps

1. Press interact at the relay to signal WOLF.
2. Step back. WOLF takes the surge.
3. The door opens.

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- He walks up to the relay's left side and takes the live contact, his nose at its edge.
- His joints whir as he answers and as he moves off.
- The **COOLANT FAULT** tag fades away.
- The rolling door winds up into its hood. Part way up, a heavier striped inner door slides sideways into the wall. The doorway opens once both are clear.

</Details>

### Route 2: you crank it

1. Go to the **MANUAL BYPASS** wheel to the right of the relay.
2. Hold interact (**HOLD E**, **CRANK** on touch) for about two seconds.
3. Keep holding until it locks over. The door opens.

- **Hard to hold?** Turn on [TOGGLE HOLDS](/docs/controls/input/#toggle-holds): one press does it.
- **Turns stay done** if you let go.
- **You can crank on route 1 too.** It just takes longer than letting WOLF take the relay.
- **You never control WOLF.** Both routes open the door.

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- The engineer steps up beside the wheel and leans into it with both hands on its rim. It gives with a soft click the moment you press.
- Coolant hisses from the relay, louder and brighter as the turns add up, and sprays onto the floor from its relief pipe.
- An alarm sounds while you crank. The row of lights under **MANUAL BYPASS** counts the turns.
- WOLF stands back from the relay while you work.
- When it locks over, the hiss and the alarm stop, the **COOLANT FAULT** tag fades and the door rolls up, with the inner door sliding aside behind it.
- If WOLF offered to help, the hint keeps counting your turns and says you can still let him take the relay. He waits back from it, ready.
- The door stands in a bolted steel blast frame under a **RESTRICTED AREA** sign. While the corridor is locked down, two caged red lamps flash like beacons. With **REDUCED MOTION** on they stay lit and steady.

</Details>

## 4. Use the safe point

**Do this:**

1. Walk through the door to the green **SAFE POINT**.
2. Press interact. A chirp confirms the save.
3. Press interact again to enter Records Access.

- **This is your first save.** It turns on **Continue** on the title screen.
- **If the save fails,** the message asks you to use the safe point again.
- **Next room:** [Records Access](/docs/walkthrough/records-access/).

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- The safe point room is shown cut open along its near side. The door is the only way in.
- While you walk through, WOLF follows no further than the relay's left side.
- Once you are at the safe point, he comes to the open doorway and faces you, in front of the **MANUAL BYPASS** wheel.
- If you step back into the doorway, he waits by the relay again. Edging about just short of the safe point does not send him back and forth.
- The lights switch over with a clunk, and WOLF recalls your answer from this run.
- The save stores positions, the door, the display name and WOLF's memory. Entering Records Access saves again.

</Details>
