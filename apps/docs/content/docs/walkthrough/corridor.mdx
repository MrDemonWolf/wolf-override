## 1. Read the corridor

After the opening, a short card names the engineer; it fades by itself, and you can move straight away. Move the engineer with **A/D** or the arrow keys. An interaction prompt appears near a station. The engineer starts beside the red purge display at the far left. Reading it with **E** is optional context and is not needed to open the door; WOLF sums it up when you walk past (or at the breaker, if you get there before you have had time to read the opening lines), so you can head straight right to the breaker.

These are default keys. If you changed bindings, use the inputs shown by the game. See [Input](/docs/controls/input/).

## 2. Arm the breaker

Move right to the amber **BREAKER**. Interact to hear WOLF ask about the relay's **COOLANT FAULT** tag. Choose answer **1** or **2**, then interact again to arm power.

The answer changes his response at the relay and is remembered later. Repeated station use does not add duplicate consequences.

## 3. Open the sealed door

Continue right to the blue **COOLANT RELAY**. The wheel on its right is the **MANUAL BYPASS**.

### WOLF-assisted route

If you chose **1**, telling WOLF about the risk, interact at the relay to signal him. The engineer steps back while he walks to his contact and takes the live relay himself. He holds it while the red seal slides up into the top of its frame, and the doorway opens once it has.

### Engineer-only route

If you chose **2**, pressing WOLF to act, he refuses, and the relay's hint points you to the **MANUAL BYPASS**. Hold interact at the handle (**HOLD E** by default, **CRANK** on touch) for about three seconds; the engineer steps up beside the wheel to turn it. Coolant hisses from the relay and an alarm sounds while you crank, the row of lights under **MANUAL BYPASS** counts the turns, and turns you have made stay done if you let go. WOLF stands back from the relay while you work. When it locks over, the seal slides open.

The handle works on both routes, so you can also crank it yourself after WOLF offers; it just takes longer than letting him take the relay. Both routes open the door. You never switch to controlling WOLF.

## 4. Use the safe point

Walk through the door to the green **SAFE POINT** and interact. WOLF recalls the answer from this run. The checkpoint saves positions, door state, display name, and memory; if it could not be written, the message asks you to use the safe point again.

Interact again to enter Records Access, which saves as well. A valid checkpoint enables **Continue** on the title screen. For the next room, open the [Records Access guide](/docs/walkthrough/records-access/).
