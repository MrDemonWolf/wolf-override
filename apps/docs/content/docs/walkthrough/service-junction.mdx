**What this page is for:** how to get through the Service Junction, step by step.

**Route:** Overload breaker → Valve → Get clear → Tank release → Exit bolt.

- **The engineer has no weapon,** only the building.
- **No new buttons.** Move, interact (tapped or held) and the two answers cover everything.
- **Hazards can knock you down.** **RETRY** sends you back to the room's last save. See [Knockdowns](/docs/controls/saves/#knockdowns).
- **The room saves four times.** See [what Continue brings back](/docs/controls/saves/#service-junction-what-continue-brings-back).

<Details>
<Summary>Entering the room, and the hints if you wait</Summary>

- You come in through a plain steel swing door. Its closer swings it shut behind you, and its lamp turns red.
- The service line toward Archive is sealed. Something hums behind the steel blast door ahead, which has a red lamp on its near column.
- The hint line says **Get through the sealed door.**
- WOLF runs ahead to the valve, reads its **LINE** gauge, warns you not to stand in front of the door when it goes, then follows you again.
- If you don't arm the breaker, help comes a little at a time. After about 45 seconds WOLF looks at the breaker while its lamp pulses. After about a minute and a half the engineer says the breaker feeds that line. After about three minutes the engineer says the whole way, and the hint line gives it too.
- Arming the breaker ends these hints. Continue from this room's first save gives the whole way at once.
- A press that can't work yet still answers: the machine's lamp flickers twice, a dull clunk plays and a line says why and what to do instead. The valve, for example, says it is dead until the breaker is armed. Press again and the box blinks as the line comes back.
- The sealed door answers for itself once you walk up to it, and keeps answering after you let go, until you move again.
- Walking into the sealed door gives a dull thud and a flicker of its seam lamp. Only the first bump says why.

</Details>

## 1. Arm the overload breaker

**Do this:**

1. Press interact at the **OVERLOAD BREAKER** by the entrance.
2. Go straight to the next step. You have about six seconds.

- **The LINE gauge** over the valve climbs as pressure builds.
- **Steam from the relief vent** means the peak is close.

<Callout type="warn" title="Don't stand on the vent grate">
  If the gauge peaks before the door is charged, the relief vent lets go. Standing over its grate then is a knockdown. Reset the breaker and try again: you lose only this attempt.
</Callout>

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- The vent's grate glows warmer, and the hiss and the pump's pitch rise.
- The vent only steams once the needle is past the middle. In the last second the grate flashes red and the hiss jumps.
- The steam rises from inside the grate, so the grate stays in view.
- When the vent lets go, the breaker trips. The needle stays pinned in red for a moment, then falls back.
- The hint warns you while you stand on the grate.
- WOLF steps back to wait by the entrance, clear of the vent.

</Details>

## 2. Charge the door

**Do this:**

1. Walk past the vent to the **VALVE** by the door.
2. Hold interact (**CRANK** on touch).
3. Keep holding for three turns. The label counts them: **VALVE 1 / 3**.

- **How cranking works:** [Hold to crank](/docs/controls/input/#hold-to-crank).
- **Turns stay done** if you let go, pause or step away.
- **The card reads CRANK THE VALVE** until the door is charged.

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- The engineer steps to the left of the wheel, facing it, and starts cranking once there.
- The wheel gives a little with a soft click the moment you press.
- Then each 0.4 seconds held is one turn: the wheel turns a quarter, a click sounds and the **VALVE** label counts it.

</Details>

## 3. Get clear of the door

On the third turn, the fuse is lit. **You have about two and a half seconds** (five ticks).

**Do this:**

1. Back away **left**, past the valve.
2. Get off the red stripes on the floor. You don't need to reach the vent.
3. Wait. The door blows open.

- **Warning signs:** a klaxon, a flashing door lamp, **FUSE LIT** on the valve and **GET CLEAR OF THE DOOR** on the card.
- **The stripes are the blast zone.** They pulse with each tick.
- **Still on the stripes when it blows?** You are knocked down. **RETRY** returns you to the room's entry save, with the door sealed again.
- **WOLF** moves clear on his own.

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- The door's lamp flashes red and white (steady red with **Reduced Motion**).
- The stripes run from just left of the valve through the doorway. A tick sounds with each pulse, higher each time.
- The steel door is torn off and thrown along the floor beyond. Rubble lands at the foot of its column.
- The breaker and valve read **BREAKER USED** and **VALVE USED**, and the game saves.
- Knocked down facing the door, the engineer is thrown back from it and lies clear of its column.
- Something whirs awake past the doorway. The card reads **GO THROUGH THE DOOR**, then **DROP THE TANK**.
- WOLF waits beside the valve, clear of the doorway. If you stand where he would wait, he waits by the entrance facing you until you move on.

</Details>

## 4. Decide how the tank comes down

Past the doorway, a **sentry** machine patrols. A coolant tank hangs over a striped mark on its path. The **TANK RELEASE** drops it.

**Do this:**

1. Walk onto the rubble line. WOLF asks how to bring the sentry down.
2. Choose an answer:
   - **1, "Draw it under the tank if you can stay clear."**
   - **2, "Stay back, I'll time it."**
3. Go on to step 5.

| Answer | What WOLF does |
| --- | --- |
| **1**, and you warned him at the corridor relay | He draws the sentry onto the mark for you |
| **1**, and you didn't | He refuses, says why and stays back. You time it yourself. |
| **2** | He stays back. You time it yourself. |

- **Either way the route goes on.** Nothing is lost.
- **Everything waits** for your answer.

### How the sentry sees you

- **Its light shows its mood:** dark when off, amber on patrol, red when it has seen you.
- **Its beam shows how far it sees.** It cannot see past solid things, like the **EXIT BOLT** box or a tank on the floor.
- **The tinted floor beside the TANK RELEASE is safe.** Standing anywhere on the tint hides you.
- **One step past the tint's right end** puts you in the open.

<Callout type="warn" title="If the sentry sees you">
  It stops for about half a second, turns red and sounds an alarm. Then it chases, faster than you walk. Turn back and get over the rubble: it won't follow past it.
</Callout>

<Details>
<Summary>More about the sentry and the tank</Summary>

- The beam ends on a bright edge where its sight ends. A pale centre line and dashed edges keep it visible whatever its colour.
- The beam stops at the rubble line and leaves a gap over the spot beside the release.
- At the far end of its patrol it passes behind the **EXIT BOLT** box and the cable box beside it.
- It is solid: you can't walk through it, even while it pauses. Walking into it makes it see you.
- When it reaches you, you are knocked down where you touch.
- A chase stops just short of the release, but from there it still reaches someone standing at it.
- It gives up a couple of seconds after you are back over the rubble.
- The tank's mark is exactly as wide as the tank, with a short upright at each end. The tank's shadow darkens the stripes as it falls.
- The engineer works the release from its right.
- On answer 1, if WOLF draws the sentry, the engineer waits at the rubble line while he dashes past. He runs to the far end of its patrol by the exit door. The sentry backs away from him onto the mark and stops there, facing him, its back to the release. He runs under the hanging tank and bounds over the sentry.
- If a line is still showing when you reach the rubble line, the engineer stops, that line comes up, and WOLF asks once it has been read.

</Details>

## 5. Drop the tank

**Do this:**

1. Stand at the **TANK RELEASE**, on the tinted floor.
2. Watch the mark. It lights up while the sentry is far enough onto it to be hit.
3. Press interact (**DROP** on touch) as the sentry comes onto the mark, a little early: the tank takes about half a second to fall.

- **WOLF drawing it?** Wait until the line says the sentry has stopped on the mark and the hint offers the drop. Then drop. Too early, and the release tells you to wait.
- **A hit** crushes the sentry for good. The release reads **RELEASE USED**, **TANK DOWN** shows, and the game saves.

### If you miss

1. Get back over the rubble. The sentry locks on and comes for the release.
2. Keep out of the coolant cloud over the tank for about three seconds. Walking into it is a knockdown.
3. Wait for the winch to haul the tank back up (**WINCHING UP**). You can drop again about six seconds after the first drop.

<Details>
<Summary>More about drops and misses</Summary>

- **The hit rule:** the tank hits if at least a quarter of the sentry's body is over the mark when it lands. So a tank that catches the sentry's near end still counts. One that only brushes it is a miss.
- **The drop window on patrol:** the sentry turns just past the mark, so each time it comes by it is over the mark for a little under a second.
- **The drop window in a chase:** a chasing sentry crosses the mark in about a third of a second.
- While WOLF draws it, the sentry watches only him: it faces away from the release with its eye red and shows no beam. If it turns on you instead, WOLF keeps clear and the release works at once.
- After a miss while WOLF draws it, he breaks off. If he is past the sentry, he stays by the exit door and its patrol turns short of him. If not, he goes back beside the valve and waits facing you.
- A missed tank is dented and lies on the empty mark, spraying coolant. While it is down, the sentry can't drive through it and waits at its edge.
- The line after a miss comes in two boxes. The second, telling you to get back over the rubble, is left out if you are already there. If WOLF was drawing the sentry, a third says he is out.
- Knocked down, the engineer slumps forward and the sentry rolls back off them. Caught by the cloud facing the tank, they stagger back from it as they fall.
- The knockdown line tells you why: seen out in the open (stay close to the release), or caught after its eyes went red (only the rubble is safe).
- Run down out in the open while facing away from the release, the engineer ends up lying on the floor past the right-hand end of the tint.

</Details>

## 6. Pop the exit bolt

**Do this:**

1. With the sentry down, go to the **EXIT BOLT** box on the right. Its plate now reads **EXIT READY**.
2. Press interact from its left side. The exit door slides open.
3. Walk up to the open door and press interact to leave.

- **The game saves** as you leave, and a short closing scene plays.
- **WOLF goes first.** The engineer follows him through.

<Details>
<Summary>What you'll see</Summary>

- WOLF goes to wait by the exit door, clear of the tank. If he was back beside the valve, the empty arm winches up first and he bounds over the crushed tank, in one arc that also carries him over the engineer if they are still at the release.
- The conduit into the cable box beside the **EXIT BOLT** box lights up, and the plate changes from **EXIT LOCKED** to **EXIT READY**.
- The panel whines, the bolt arcs and drops, and the exit door slides into its wall pocket. Its plate reads **EXIT OPENING**, then its lamp turns green.
- WOLF waits in the doorway, looking back at you. From the **EXIT BOLT** box the hint only says the door is open.
- When you leave, the lights switch over with a clunk. Both pass behind the wall's near column.
- Continue from the sentry save starts WOLF by the door.

</Details>

## Continue to the annex

1. Leave through the open exit after the closing lines.
2. Continue into the **MAINTENANCE ANNEX**.
3. Follow the [annex guide](/docs/walkthrough/maintenance-annex/) for the next interaction steps.

- The annex uses existing room art as provisional staging.
- Its records can be checked through the engineer's own access.
- The chapter's end card comes after the annex, not at this door.
