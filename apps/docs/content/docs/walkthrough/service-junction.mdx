The service line toward Archive is sealed, and something hums behind the door. The engineer has no weapon, only the building. WOLF runs ahead to the valve in front of the door, reads its **LINE** gauge and backs off; then he follows you again.

The whole room is playable, from the entry hatch to the exit hatch. What lies past the exit hatch is not built yet. The timings below come from the game's rules and automated checks; the room has not yet been played through by hand, so they may be tuned.

You need no new buttons here: move, the interact button (tapped or held) and the two answers cover everything. A knockdown only ever sends you back to the room's last checkpoint. Each automatic save that works shows **Checkpoint saved.** in the hint line for a moment.

## 1. Arm the overload breaker

Interact with the **OVERLOAD BREAKER** by the entrance. The line starts to build: the needle on the small dial over the valve, captioned **LINE**, climbs, the grate of the relief vent between the breaker and the valve glows warmer, and the hiss turns brighter and louder while the pump's pitch rises with it. WOLF steps back to wait by the entrance, well clear of the vent.

The gauge takes about six seconds to peak. The vent only starts to steam once the needle is past the middle, so steam means the peak is close; in the last second the grate flashes red and the hiss jumps. If the gauge peaks before the door is charged, the **relief vent lets go** and the breaker trips; the needle stays pinned at the top in red for a moment, then falls back. Having any part of the engineer over the vent grate when it lets go is a knockdown; the hint warns you while you stand there. Reset the breaker and try again; nothing is lost but this attempt.

## 2. Charge the door

Walk past the vent to the **VALVE** by the door and **hold** the interact button (**E**, controller **A**, or the touch button, which reads **CRANK** here). The engineer steps to the left of the wheel, facing it, and starts cranking once there. The wheel gives a little with a soft click the moment you press, then each 0.4 seconds held is one real turn: the wheel turns a quarter, a click sounds and the **VALVE** label counts it (**VALVE 1 / 3**). With **TOGGLE HOLDS** on in [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#controls), one press starts the crank and it keeps turning until you press again or walk away. Three turns charge the door; the card reads **CRANK THE VALVE** until then. Turns you have made survive letting go, pausing or stepping away.

## 3. Get clear of the door

On the third turn a klaxon sounds, the seam down the pressure bulkhead strobes white, the **VALVE** label reads **FUSE LIT** and the card says **GET CLEAR OF THE DOOR**. Red hazard stripes pulse on the floor in front of the door, twice a second, and a tick sounds with each pulse, a little higher each time: that striped stretch, from just left of the valve through the doorway, is the blast zone. You have about two and a half seconds (five ticks): back away **left**, past the valve and off the stripes. You do not need to go as far as the vent. WOLF moves clear on his own. Then the door goes.

If you are still on the stripes when it blows, you see the door blow out, you are knocked down and the game returns to the checkpoint made when you entered the room, with the door sealed again. Otherwise the bulkhead buckles and is left torn open on the passage beyond, the rubble lands, the breaker and valve read **SPENT**, and the game saves. You can walk through the doorway; its near pillar passes in front of you and turns half see-through while you are behind it.

WOLF heads up to the rubble and waits there for the engineer's call. The card reads **CHECK THE LANE** until he brings up the tank, then **DROP THE TANK**.

## 4. Decide how the tank comes down

Past the rubble is a lane patrolled by a sentry machine. Its scanner light shows what it is doing: amber while it patrols, red once it has locked on. Its beam shows how far it sees. It stops at the rubble line and leaves a gap over the alcove, where it cannot see you. A coolant tank hangs over the lane from an overhead arm, above a striped **floor mark** with a bracket at each end, exactly as wide as the tank; the tank's shadow darkens the stripes as it falls. The **TANK RELEASE** that drops it stands at the left end of an alcove that starts right at the rubble line; the engineer works it from its right, still inside the alcove. Its floor is tinted, with a short upright post at each end; standing anywhere between the two posts hides you from the sentry, and a step past the right-hand post puts you back in its lane, where it can see you, and its patrol turns before it reaches you there.

When you step onto the rubble line, WOLF asks how to bring the sentry down, and everything waits for your answer:

- **1, "Draw it under the tank if you can stay clear."** If you told WOLF about the relay risk in the corridor, he chooses to do it, and says so, and you let him go first: the engineer waits at the rubble line for a moment while he dashes past. He runs down the lane to its far end by the exit hatch. The sentry turns to him and backs away ahead of him, never coming at him, as far as the far end of its patrol; he ducks under the hanging tank, catches it there and bounds over it, and it backs onto the mark and stops right on it, facing him with its back to the alcove. If you did not, he refuses, says why, and stays at the rubble. Either way the route continues; nothing is lost.
- **2, "Stay back, I'll time it."** WOLF stays at the rubble and you time the drop yourself.

In the lane, the sentry sees you if you are ahead of it and within the reach of its beam (anywhere but the alcove), or if you walk into it. Once it has seen you it stops for about half a second while its light turns red and a short alarm sounds, then it chases faster than you walk, and touching it is a knockdown. A chase stops at the mouth of the alcove, but from there it still reaches someone standing at the release. Use that pause to turn back. Get back over the rubble: it will not follow you past it, and it gives up a couple of seconds later. To reach the alcove safely, cross when it is heading away from you.

## 5. Drop the tank

At the **TANK RELEASE** interact (the touch button reads **DROP**). The clamp lets go and the tank falls free onto the floor mark; it takes about half a second to land. It hits if at least a quarter of the sentry's body is inside the brackets when it lands, so a tank that catches the sentry's near end still counts; one that only brushes it is a miss. On patrol the sentry turns just past the mark, so each time it comes by it is over the brackets for about one second; when it is chasing it crosses them in about a third of a second. The brackets light up while the sentry is far enough inside them to be hit.

- If WOLF is drawing the sentry, it watches only him: it faces away from the alcove, toward him at the far end of the lane, with its eye red, and stops scanning, so no beam shows. The release waits until he is at his spot and has it stopped on the mark; press it too early and it tells you to wait. The moment it stops, the line says so and the hint offers the drop: drop then. If it has turned on you, WOLF keeps clear of it and the release works at once. A miss then makes him break off for this attempt: once he is past the sentry he stays by the exit hatch, and its patrol turns short of him; if he was not past it yet, he goes back to the rubble.
- On your own, drop as the sentry comes onto the mark, a little early to allow for the fall.

A hit crushes the sentry under the tank for good, the release reads **RELEASE SPENT**, **TANK DOWN** shows over the tank, and the game saves. A miss dents the tank, which lies on the empty mark for a moment, spraying a cloud of coolant over it for about three seconds (walking into it is a knockdown), and the sentry locks on (the same short pause) and comes for the release: get back over the rubble. While the tank is down the sentry cannot drive through it and waits at its edge. Then the winch hauls the tank back up (**WINCHING UP**); the clamp latches about six seconds after the drop, and only then can you drop it again.

If the sentry runs you down after spotting you out in its lane, the engineer falls away from the alcove, past its right-hand post, the sentry rolls back off them, and the knockdown card says it caught you past the alcove's right post; if a missed drop turned it on you, or it reached you inside the alcove, the card says that once its eyes go red only the rubble is safe.

## 6. Pop the exit bolt

With the sentry down, WOLF goes to wait by the exit hatch, clear of the tank. If he was back at the rubble, the empty arm first winches up into its ceiling plate and he bounds clean over the crushed tank on his way, in one arc that carries him over the engineer too if they are still at the release; Continue from this checkpoint starts him by the hatch. The conduit along the wall from the **TANK RELEASE** to the **EXIT BOLT** box lights up: the release's power now runs to the bolt. Go to the box on the right and interact from its left side. The panel whines, the bolt arcs and drops, and the hatch slides open; its plate reads **EXIT OPENING** until it has. Interact again to leave: the room's lights switch over with a clunk, the game saves and a short closing scene plays, then WOLF goes through the hatch first and the engineer follows him out.

## End of the slice

An **END OF SLICE** card thanks you for playing and recaps three choices from your run: what happened at the relay, who copied the mirror timestamp and how the sentry came down. Interact (or tap or click its button, or press Back or Pause) to go on to the title. On a finished save the title shows one **PLAY AGAIN** button in place of New Game and Continue; it starts a fresh run with the opening, and the finished save stays until the new run saves over it.

If you are stuck, [troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/) has recovery tips. [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/) explains which routes have automated checks and which still need hands-on play.
