---
title: Service Junction
description: Optional help for the breaker, the valve, the sealed door, the sentry lane and the exit bolt in the third source room.
---

**Puzzle solutions for the third source room follow.** [Records Access](/docs/walkthrough/records-access/) comes first; its exit leads here once the first copy is secured.

The service line toward Archive is sealed, and something hums behind the door. The engineer has no weapon, only the building. WOLF runs ahead, reads the pressure behind the door and backs off; then he follows you again.

The whole room is playable, from the entry hatch to the exit hatch. What lies past the exit hatch is not built yet.

## 1. Arm the overload breaker

Interact with the **OVERLOAD BREAKER** on the left. The line starts to build: the gauge over the valve climbs, the relief vent between the breaker and the valve steams harder, and the hiss and pump pitch rise with it.

The gauge takes about six seconds to peak. If it peaks before the seal is charged, the **relief vent lets go**, the breaker trips and the gauge empties. Having any part of the engineer over the vent grate when it lets go is a knockdown; the hint warns you while you stand there. Reset the breaker and try again; nothing is lost but this attempt.

## 2. Charge the seal

Walk past the vent to the **VALVE** by the door and **hold** the interact button (**E**, controller **A**, or the touch button, which reads **CRANK** here). Each 0.4 seconds held is one real turn: the wheel turns a quarter, a click sounds and one of three lights goes green. Three turns charge the seal. Turns you have made survive letting go, pausing or stepping away.

## 3. Clear the door

On the third turn a klaxon sounds, the seam strobes white and the card says **CLEAR THE DOOR**. You have one second: walk **left**, out of the space between the vent and the door. Anywhere at or left of the vent is clear. Then the door goes.

If you are still in front of the door when it blows, you are knocked down and the game returns to the autosave made when you entered the room, with the door sealed again. Otherwise the door is down, the rubble lands, the breaker and valve read **SPENT**, and the game saves.

WOLF holds at the rubble and will not cross it on his own.

## 4. Decide how the tank comes down

Past the rubble is a lane patrolled by a sentry machine. Its eye strip shows what it is doing: amber while it patrols, red once it has locked on. A coolant tank hangs over the lane from an overhead arm, above a striped **floor mark**; the **ARM PANEL** that drops it sits in an alcove just past the rubble. Standing in that alcove hides you from the sentry.

When you step onto the rubble line, WOLF asks how to bring the sentry down, and everything waits for your answer:

- **1, "Draw it under the arm if you can stay clear."** If you told WOLF about the relay risk in the corridor, he chooses to do it: he runs past the mark and the sentry turns on him and stops right on the mark. If you did not, he refuses and stays at the rubble. Either way the route continues; nothing is lost.
- **2, "Stay back, I'll time it."** WOLF stays at the rubble and you time the drop yourself.

In the lane, the sentry sees you if you are ahead of it and within a few body lengths, or if you walk into it. Once it has seen you it chases faster than you walk, and touching it is a knockdown. Get back over the rubble: it will not follow you past it, and it gives up a couple of seconds later. To reach the alcove safely, cross when it is heading away from you.

## 5. Drop the tank

At the **ARM PANEL** interact (the touch button reads **DROP**). The clamp lets go and the tank falls onto the floor mark; it takes about half a second to land, and whatever is on the stripes when it lands is hit.

- If WOLF is holding the sentry on the mark, drop as soon as it stops.
- On your own, drop as the sentry comes onto the mark, a little early to allow for the fall.

A hit knocks the sentry over for good, and the game saves. A miss dents the tank, which vents a cloud of coolant over the mark for about three seconds (walking into it is a knockdown), and the sentry comes straight for the panel: get back over the rubble. If WOLF was drawing it, he breaks off and will not try again on this attempt. The arm then winches the tank back up for about six seconds before you can drop it again.

## 6. Pop the exit bolt

With the sentry down, go to the **EXIT BOLT** box on the right and interact. The panel whines, the bolt arcs and drops, and the hatch slides open. Interact again to leave: the game saves, a short closing scene plays, and you return to the title. **Continue** brings you back to the open hatch.
