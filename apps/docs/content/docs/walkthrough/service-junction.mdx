**Puzzle solutions for the third source room follow.** [Records Access](/docs/walkthrough/records-access/) comes first; its exit leads here once the first copy is secured.

The service line toward Archive is sealed, and something hums behind the door. The engineer has no weapon, only the building. WOLF runs ahead, reads the pressure behind the door and backs off; then he follows you again.

The room is in progress: the breaker, the valve and the door blast are playable; what waits past the door is still being built.

## 1. Arm the overload breaker

Interact with the **OVERLOAD BREAKER** on the left. The line starts to build: the gauge over the valve climbs, the relief vent between the breaker and the valve steams harder, and the hiss and pump pitch rise with it.

The gauge takes about six seconds to peak. If it peaks before the seal is charged, the **relief vent lets go**, the breaker trips and the gauge empties. Having any part of the engineer over the vent grate when it lets go is a knockdown; the hint warns you while you stand there. Reset the breaker and try again; nothing is lost but this attempt.

## 2. Charge the seal

Walk past the vent to the **VALVE** by the door and **hold** the interact button (**E**, controller **A**, or the touch button, which reads **CRANK** here). Each 0.4 seconds held is one real turn: the wheel turns a quarter, a click sounds and one of three lights goes green. Three turns charge the seal. Turns you have made survive letting go, pausing or stepping away.

## 3. Clear the door

On the third turn a klaxon sounds, the seam strobes white and the card says **CLEAR THE DOOR**. You have one second: walk **left**, out of the space between the vent and the door. Anywhere at or left of the vent is clear. Then the door goes.

If you are still in front of the door when it blows, you are knocked down and the game returns to the autosave made when you entered the room, with the door sealed again. Otherwise the door is down, the rubble lands, the breaker and valve read **SPENT**, and the game saves.

WOLF holds at the rubble and will not cross it yet. The lane past the door is the next part of this room and is not finished.

If you are stuck, [troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/) has recovery tips. [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/) explains which routes have automated checks and which still need hands-on play.
