The service line toward Archive is sealed, and something hums behind the door. The engineer has no weapon, only the building. On the way in the engineer says only that the door is the way through, and the hint line says **Get through the sealed door.** WOLF runs ahead to the valve in front of the door and reads its **LINE** gauge, warns you not to stand in front of the door when it goes, and then follows you again.

If you have not armed the breaker after a while, the room helps a little at a time: after about 45 seconds WOLF stops and looks at the breaker while its lamp pulses; after about a minute and a half the engineer says the breaker by the entrance feeds that line; after about three minutes the engineer says the whole way, in two short lines, and the hint line gives it too. Arming the breaker ends these hints. Continue from this room's first checkpoint gives the whole way at once.

The whole room is playable, from the entry hatch to the exit hatch. What lies past the exit hatch is not built yet. The timings below come from the game's rules and automated checks; the room has not yet been played through by hand, so they may be tuned.

You need no new buttons here: move, the interact button (tapped or held) and the two answers cover everything. A knockdown only ever sends you back to the room's last checkpoint. Each automatic save that works shows **Checkpoint saved.** in the hint line for a moment.

A press that cannot do anything yet still answers: the machine's lamp flickers twice, a short dull clunk plays and a line in the dialogue box says why and what to do instead (the valve, for example, says it is dead until the breaker is armed). Press again and the box blinks as the line comes back. The sealed door answers for itself once you walk up to it, even after you let go, until you move again, and walking into it gives a dull thud and a flicker of its seam lamp; only the first bump says why.

## 1. Arm the overload breaker

Interact with the **OVERLOAD BREAKER** by the entrance. The line starts to build: the needle on the small dial over the valve, captioned **LINE**, climbs, the grate of the relief vent between the breaker and the valve glows warmer, and the hiss turns brighter and louder while the pump's pitch rises with it. WOLF steps back to wait by the entrance, well clear of the vent.

The gauge takes about six seconds to peak. The vent only starts to steam once the needle is past the middle, so steam means the peak is close; in the last second the grate flashes red and the hiss jumps. The steam rises from inside the grate, so the grate stays in plain view through it. If the gauge peaks before the door is charged, the **relief vent lets go** and the breaker trips; the needle stays pinned at the top in red for a moment, then falls back. Having any part of the engineer over the vent grate when it lets go is a knockdown; the hint warns you while you stand there. Reset the breaker and try again; nothing is lost but this attempt.

## 2. Charge the door

Walk past the vent to the **VALVE** by the door and **hold** the interact button (**E**, controller **A**, or the touch button, which reads **CRANK** here). The engineer steps to the left of the wheel, facing it, and starts cranking once there. The wheel gives a little with a soft click the moment you press, then each 0.4 seconds held is one real turn: the wheel turns a quarter, a click sounds and the **VALVE** label counts it (**VALVE 1 / 3**). With **TOGGLE HOLDS** on in [Settings](/docs/controls/settings/#controls), one press starts the crank and it keeps turning until you press again or walk away. Three turns charge the door; the card reads **CRANK THE VALVE** until then. Turns you have made survive letting go, pausing or stepping away.

## 3. Get clear of the door

On the third turn a klaxon sounds, the seam down the pressure bulkhead strobes white, the **VALVE** label reads **FUSE LIT** and the card says **GET CLEAR OF THE DOOR**. Red hazard stripes pulse on the floor in front of the door, twice a second, and a tick sounds with each pulse, a little higher each time: that striped stretch, from just left of the valve through the doorway, is the blast zone. You have about two and a half seconds (five ticks): back away **left**, past the valve and off the stripes. You do not need to go as far as the vent. WOLF moves clear on his own. Then the door goes.

If you are still on the stripes when it blows, you see the door blow out and you are knocked down (facing the door, the engineer is thrown back from it as they slump, so they lie clear of its frame), and the game returns to the checkpoint made when you entered the room, with the door sealed again. Otherwise the bulkhead buckles and is left torn open on the passage beyond, the rubble lands, the breaker and valve read **BREAKER USED** and **VALVE USED**, and the game saves. You can walk through the doorway; its near pillar passes in front of you and turns half see-through while you are behind it.

WOLF heads up towards the rubble and waits for the engineer's call on the floor beside the valve, the nearest open floor on this side of it, so he never stands inside the valve or the torn doorway. If you are still standing just past the valve, where he would wait, he waits by the entrance, facing you, until you move on; edging about near the valve does not send him back and forth. Something whirs awake past the doorway. The card reads **GO THROUGH THE DOOR** until he brings up the tank, then **DROP THE TANK**.

## 4. Decide how the tank comes down

Past the rubble a sentry machine patrols the floor beyond the doorway. Its scanner light and the round lamp on its front show what it is doing and which way it faces: dark while it is powered down, amber while it patrols, red once it has locked on. Its beam shows how far it sees, its sights: it ends on a bright edge exactly where its sight ends, and a pale line down its middle and dashed edges keep it visible whatever its colour. Anything solid in its way stops the beam, and the sentry cannot see past it: the **EXIT BOLT** box, and the tank while it lies on the floor. At the far end of its patrol it passes behind the **EXIT BOLT** box and the cable box beside it. The beam stops at the rubble line and leaves a gap over the spot beside the **TANK RELEASE**, where it cannot see you. A coolant tank hangs from an overhead arm above a striped mark on the floor, exactly as wide as the tank, with a short upright at each end; the tank's shadow darkens the stripes as it falls. The **TANK RELEASE** that drops it stands just past the rubble line; the engineer works it from its right. The floor there is tinted, with a short upright at each end of the tint: standing anywhere on the tint hides you from the sentry, and a step past its right-hand end puts you out in the open, where it can see you, and its patrol turns before it reaches you there.

When you step onto the rubble line, WOLF asks how to bring the sentry down, and everything waits for your answer. If the line after the blast still has a box to show, the engineer stops at the rubble line, that box comes up at once, and he asks once it has been up its reading time:

- **1, "Draw it under the tank if you can stay clear."** If you told WOLF about the relay risk in the corridor, he chooses to do it, and says so, and you let him go first: the engineer waits at the rubble line for a moment while he dashes past. He runs past it to the far end of its patrol, by the exit hatch. The sentry turns to him and backs away ahead of him, never coming at him, as far as the far end of its patrol; he ducks under the hanging tank, catches it there and bounds over it, and it backs onto the mark and stops right on it, facing him with its back to the **TANK RELEASE**. If you did not, he refuses, says why, and stays back beside the valve. Either way the route continues; nothing is lost.
- **2, "Stay back, I'll time it."** WOLF stays back beside the valve and you time the drop yourself.

Past the rubble, the sentry sees you if you are ahead of it and within the reach of its beam (anywhere but the tint beside the release), or if you walk into it. Once it has seen you it stops for about half a second while its light turns red and a short alarm sounds, then it chases faster than you walk; when it reaches you, you are knocked down where the two of you touch. It is solid: you cannot walk through it, even while it pauses. A chase stops just short of the release, but from there it still reaches someone standing at it. Use that pause to turn back. Get back over the rubble: it will not follow you past it, and it gives up a couple of seconds later. To reach the release safely, cross when it is heading away from you.

## 5. Drop the tank

At the **TANK RELEASE** interact (the touch button reads **DROP**). The clamp lets go and the tank falls free onto the mark; it takes about half a second to land. It hits if at least a quarter of the sentry's body is over the mark when it lands, so a tank that catches the sentry's near end still counts; one that only brushes it is a miss. On patrol the sentry turns just past the mark, so each time it comes by it is over it for a little under a second; when it is chasing it crosses it in about a third of a second. The mark lights up while the sentry is far enough onto it to be hit.

- If WOLF is drawing the sentry, it watches only him: it faces away from the release, toward him at the far end of its patrol, with its eye red, and stops scanning, so no beam shows. The release waits until he is at his spot and has it stopped on the mark; press it too early and it tells you to wait. The moment it stops, the line says so and the hint offers the drop: drop then. If it has turned on you, WOLF keeps clear of it and the release works at once. A miss then makes him break off for this attempt: once he is past the sentry he stays by the exit hatch, and its patrol turns short of him; if he was not past it yet, he goes back to his spot beside the valve.
- On your own, drop as the sentry comes onto the mark, a little early to allow for the fall.

A hit crushes the sentry under the tank for good, the release reads **RELEASE USED**, **TANK DOWN** shows over the tank, and the game saves. A miss dents the tank, which lies on the empty mark for a moment, spraying a cloud of coolant over it for about three seconds (walking into it is a knockdown), and the sentry locks on (the same short pause) and comes for the release: get back over the rubble. The line after a miss comes in two short boxes. The second, telling you to get back over the rubble, comes as the sentry swings toward you after its pause, and is left out if you are already back over the rubble by then. If WOLF was drawing the sentry he says in a third that he is out, and the line saying the tank is back up waits until after it. While the tank is down the sentry cannot drive through it and waits at its edge. Then the winch hauls the tank back up (**WINCHING UP**); the clamp latches about six seconds after the drop, and only then can you drop it again.

Knocked down, the engineer always slumps forward, the way they were facing, and the sentry rolls back off them; caught by the coolant cloud facing the tank, they stagger back from it as they go down, so they never lie inside it. If it runs you down after spotting you out in the open, the knockdown card says it saw you out in the open and to stay close to the **TANK RELEASE**; facing away from the release, the engineer ends up lying on the floor past the right-hand end of the tint. If a missed drop turned it on you, or it reached you at the release, the card says that once its eyes go red only the rubble is safe.

## 6. Pop the exit bolt

With the sentry down, WOLF goes to wait by the exit hatch, clear of the tank. If he was back beside the valve, the empty arm first winches up into its ceiling plate and he bounds clean over the crushed tank on his way, in one arc that carries him over the engineer too if they are still at the release; Continue from this checkpoint starts him by the hatch. The conduit that comes up out of the floor into the cable box beside the **EXIT BOLT** box lights up: the release's power, run under the floor, now reaches the bolt, and the plate over the box changes from **EXIT LOCKED** to **EXIT READY**. Go to the box on the right and interact from its left side. The panel whines, the bolt arcs and drops, and the hatch slides open; its plate reads **EXIT OPENING** until it has. Interact again to leave: the room's lights switch over with a clunk, the game saves and a short closing scene plays, then WOLF goes through the hatch first and the engineer follows him out.

## End of the slice

An **END OF SLICE** card thanks you for playing and recaps three choices from your run: what happened at the relay, who copied the mirror timestamp and how the sentry came down. Interact (or tap or click its button, or press Back or Pause) to go on to the title. On a finished save the title shows one **PLAY AGAIN** button in place of New Game and Continue; it starts a fresh run with the opening, and the finished save stays until the new run saves over it.

If you are stuck, [troubleshooting](/docs/controls/troubleshooting/) has recovery tips. [Testing and deployment](/docs/development/testing/) explains which routes have automated checks and which still need hands-on play.
