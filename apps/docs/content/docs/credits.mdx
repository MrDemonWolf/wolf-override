**What this page is for:** who made WOLF OVERRIDE, which tools, fonts and sounds it uses, and which research informed it.

## Original work

**Original game concept, core story, and creative vision:** Nathanial Henniges. WOLF's self-awakening, his independent bond with the engineer, and the evidence and accountability direction come from Nathanial's concept. **Development:** MrDemonWolf, Inc., with AI assistance directed and reviewed by Nathanial. Research references below inform craft and plausibility; they did not originate the game or its story.

- They were made from brand files supplied by Nathanial Henniges. All rights are reserved by MrDemonWolf, Inc. (see [Legal](/docs/legal/)).

- **Prompts only:** for the October 8 corridor and Records Access plates, the coolant relay and the safe point, the generation prompts are kept in private art notes; the raw outputs are not in the repository.

## Sound

- **Most sound effects** are generated by the game's own code from fixed recipes (`scripts/sfx_bank.gd`).
- **A few are recordings:** the steam heard at the corridor's **MANUAL BYPASS** and in the Service Junction, and two of WOLF's growls, are CC0 recordings processed with project scripts (see [sound recordings](#sound-recordings)).
- **AI-assisted:** the newer recipes and the recording processing came from AI-assisted sound passes.
- **Picked by ear:** Nathanial Henniges picked the earlier sounds by ear.
- **Not picked by ear yet:** the station chirp, WOLF's joint whir and the Service Junction's fuse tick, added on October 13, 2026 in AI-assisted passes.
- **No music or voice** is included in the current source build.

## Sound recordings

The steam in the corridor and the Service Junction and two of WOLF's growls start from recordings released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), processed for the game: cut, looped, filtered and, for the growls, run through a machine-like body. CC0 asks for no credit; these authors are credited with thanks.

- **Joseph Sardin, [BigSoundBank.com](https://bigsoundbank.com/) / LaSonotheque.org**: [Pressure cooker](https://bigsoundbank.com/pressure-cooker-s0805.html), [Air leak](https://bigsoundbank.com/air-leak-s1486.html) and [Air](https://bigsoundbank.com/air-s0138.html), for the hiss of the pressure line and of the corridor's **MANUAL BYPASS**, the relief vent's burst and the coolant cloud.
- **pauliuw**: [Dog sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-sounds) on OpenGameArt.org, for WOLF's growl at the door seam and the voice in his refusal growl.
- **bonebrah**: [Dog Growl](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-growl) on OpenGameArt.org, the rasp in WOLF's refusal growl.
- **AntumDeluge**: [Bear Growls](https://opengameart.org/content/bear-growls) on OpenGameArt.org, recorded by the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, a faint texture in WOLF's refusal growl.

Each file's source, licence and processing are recorded in the [asset register](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/blob/main/apps/game/assets/manifest.json).

<Summary>Full notes on the provisional art</Summary>

- The provisional WOLF, engineer, and Director sprites and the first title image were generated with ChatGPT Create.
- In October 2026 the Codex built-in image generation tool redrew WOLF's head in that title image to remove an extra ear, and redrew the three sprites.
- Using the earlier ChatGPT Create room paintings and machine sheets as references, it produced the corridor and Records Access as layered gameplay rooms (far, middle and near plates) with eight station props (some of them replaced on October 16, 2026 by whole pieces, see below).
- Using those approved sets as style references, it then produced the Service Junction's layered room, its station machines and the sentry (the machines and the sentry replaced on October 16, 2026).
- It painted the current title image as an edit of the first one, with the redrawn sprites and the corridor layers as references.
- **The corridor's door end** (the concrete wall across the corridor, its rolling steel door, the wall-mounted manual bypass, the machinery room beyond and the plinths under the relay and the safe point) was made design first in October 2026:
  - its shapes were drawn by Python scripts from the measured sizes of real doors, gearboxes, conduit and cabinets;
  - the Codex built-in image generation tool painted material and light only inside those shapes, and scripts cleaned up the result;
  - its stencilled marks are typeset in Roboto;
  - its research, design notes, review rounds and the raw generated images are kept in the repository under [`docs/research-2026-10/sources/corridor-door-end-2026-10-12/`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/tree/1cf8d73c4703f8d8dfb693c41fc57c62c43118e3/docs/research-2026-10/sources/corridor-door-end-2026-10-12).
- **The door end's additions** of October 2026 (a bolted steel blast frame and the **RESTRICTED AREA** sign, an inner blast door that slides into the wall, two caged red beacons, a relief valve and discharge pipe on the coolant relay, a shorter ceiling light and the cut-away wall along the safe point's room) were made the same way:
  - the sign is typeset in Roboto;
  - the engineer's two crank frames were cut from their own sprite by a script; the image tool repainted the arms and the seams where they meet the body, and the legs are the sprite's own pixels (replaced on October 16, 2026 by whole crank pieces);
  - their research, design notes, five review rounds and the raw generated images, which keep their content credentials, are under [`docs/research-2026-10/sources/corridor-additions-2026-10-14/`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/tree/1cf8d73c4703f8d8dfb693c41fc57c62c43118e3/docs/research-2026-10/sources/corridor-additions-2026-10-14).
- **The doors of Records Access and the Service Junction** were made the same way in October 2026:
  - the two steel swing doors of Records Access and the security container beside its exit (the container replaced on October 16, 2026 by a whole piece);
  - the Service Junction's entry swing door, its blast door (whole, breaking, thrown along the floor and lying there, with the damaged doorway it leaves) and its sliding exit door with the passage beyond it;
  - their shapes were drawn by Python scripts from the measured hardware of real doors in each room's own camera;
  - the Codex built-in image generation tool in edit mode painted material and light only inside those shapes, and scripts cleaned up the result and made later fixes without new paint; none of it contains lettering;
  - their notes, review rounds, scripts, provenance and the raw generated images behind the shipped atlases are kept under [`docs/research-2026-10/sources/doors-2026-10-14/`](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolf-override/tree/1cf8d73c4703f8d8dfb693c41fc57c62c43118e3/docs/research-2026-10/sources/doors-2026-10-14);
  - Nathanial Henniges approved them on October 14, 2026.
- **The engineer's collapse** when knocked down (five frames: knees, slump, lying on the side) was painted by the same tool over drawn pose guides, with the redrawn sprites and character sheets as references (replaced on October 16, 2026, see below).
- **The characters** (WOLF; the engineer standing, cranking and knocked down; the Director; the sentry active and wrecked) were repainted on October 16, 2026 as whole pieces, one image per pose, replacing the earlier sprites and frames:
  - Python scripts drew each piece's exact shape from the earlier art as a guide;
  - the Codex built-in image generation tool in edit mode painted over those guides, one sheet per group, and scripts cut out and cleaned up each piece;
  - the lights on WOLF and the sentry are drawn by the game;
  - Nathanial Henniges approved the set on October 16, 2026.
- **The station props** (the corridor's purge display and breaker, the MANUAL BYPASS handwheel, and in the Service Junction the OVERLOAD BREAKER, VALVE, RELIEF VENT, tank arm, coolant tank, TANK RELEASE and EXIT BOLT panels; in Records Access the PURGE QUEUE, MIRROR PORT and security container) were repainted the same way on October 16, 2026, one whole image per machine:
  - the purge display, PURGE QUEUE, MIRROR PORT, tank arm and EXIT BOLT panel are drawn at their real sizes, smaller than before;
  - one handwheel serves both the MANUAL BYPASS and the VALVE, and one breaker both rooms;
  - their screens, lamps and gauge faces are painted dark and drawn by the game;
  - Nathanial Henniges approved the set on October 16, 2026.
