Checked by automated tests and still images from scripted play only, not played by hand.

Station lights, glows and name plates now draw behind the engineer and WOLF instead of over them. Checked by automated tests and still images from scripted play only, not played by hand.

The exit bolt is worked from the left of its box, and its interact reach now starts there. WOLF reads the pressure from the floor beside the valve instead of standing in it, waits by the entrance hatch with his nose at the breaker rather than inside it, and in the corridor he waits back from the relay while the engineer stands at it or at the **MANUAL BYPASS**, until he is signalled. Part way through cranking the **MANUAL BYPASS** after telling WOLF the risk, the hint keeps the turn count and still says you can let WOLF take the relay. Walking out through the Service Junction's hatch no longer shows a **CONTINUE** prompt that did nothing. Station names in the corridor and Records Access now fade out at the edge of the screen, like the junction's, instead of showing half a word.
- Website: the corridor and Service Junction walkthroughs describe the sidestep beside machines, WOLF waiting back from the relay, the wider alcove, the release's "stop" line and where the exit bolt is worked from.

While WOLF draws the sentry onto the mark, its red beam ends short of the alcove instead of running into the engineer at the release, so it no longer looks the same as being spotted. Pressing the release too early keeps its "wait" line up for about a second and a half, so the answer can be read even if the sentry stops a moment later. Arming the overload breaker steps the engineer out beside the cabinet instead of leaving them in front of it. Checked by automated tests and still images from scripted play only, not played by hand.

On a finished save the title's Continue reads **PLAY AGAIN** and starts a fresh run with the opening; like New Game, it keeps the finished save until the new run saves over it. WOLF's line on entering Records Access says plainly why he took the relay, or that you cranked the bypass after he offered.

WOLF reads the mirror port from its right instead of from inside it. Checked by automated tests and still images only, not played by hand.

## October 8, 2026

- A third room, the **Service Junction**, begins past the Records exit: after the closing beat, use the open exit again to follow the service line. Arm the **OVERLOAD BREAKER**, then **hold** interact at the **VALVE** (the touch button reads **CRANK**) for three real turns before the relief vent lets go, and get clear of the door before its one-second fuse ends. The gauge, steam, hiss, pump pitch, wheel and lights all follow the real line; WOLF reads the door seam on his own and holds at the rubble afterwards. The room is in progress: what lies past the door is not built yet.
- Being knocked down in the Service Junction (standing on the vent when it lets go, or in front of the door when it blows) returns you to the room's last autosave with your choices intact; entering the room and blowing the door both autosave. Saves are now version 4; older checkpoints still load.
- Menus, buttons and on-screen cards share a raised look with a lit top edge, soft shadows and short hover, focus and press motion; Settings keeps its squared terminal style. On touch builds the PAUSE and USE buttons stay fully on screen.
- The rooms gained depth: glowing, gently flickering ceiling lamps with soft light shafts, pulsing lockdown beacons, wall haze, drifting dust, soft reflections and contact shadows on the floor under the characters and machines, and a light vignette.
- Big moments now answer back: the containment gate letting go in the opening, the relay contact, the seal landing and the Records exit each get a short sound, a brief camera shake and a spark or dust burst; the gate and the Records exit also flash the screen. The sounds are generated by the game itself from a fixed recipe (no audio files) and follow the Master and Effects volume sliders.
- Settings > Display has a new **REDUCED MOTION** toggle that turns off camera shake and hit-stop and shortens screen flashes. It is saved with the other preferences.
- Those moments also rumble a controller while one is in use and vibrate a phone or tablet on touch play.
- The title **CHANGELOG** button now opens these updates inside the game; scroll with the mouse wheel, by dragging the text or with up/down, and use **OPEN FULL CHANGELOG ONLINE** for the website version. **BACK TO TITLE**, Esc, controller B or Android Back return to the title.
- After you tell WOLF about the relay risk, he walks to the contact himself; you no longer have to find the exact spot. Using the relay again while he is walking takes the engineer's bypass instead.
- Removed the N (new run) and L/F9 (load checkpoint) hotkeys, which discarded unsaved progress without warning. Use Pause, then Return to Title, then New Game or Continue.
- Pause during the opening now pauses instead of skipping; choose **Skip Opening** from Pause to go straight to play.
- On Android, Back backs out of menus and pauses play instead of closing the game; it only closes the game from the title screen.
- Switching away from the game during play pauses it.
- Returning to the title no longer leaves an unanswered choice that a later key press could answer.
- In-game changelog and credits links point at wolfoverride.mrdemonwolf.dev.
