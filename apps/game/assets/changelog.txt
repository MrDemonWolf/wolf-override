- New generated sounds for the sentry's motors, the arm's winch, the tank landing and the exit bolt.
- The sentry's patrol now turns before it reaches the ARM PANEL alcove, so it no longer brushes past an engineer hiding there; after giving up a chase it walks back into its patrol instead of jumping; and when WOLF draws it onto the mark it stops clear of him, with WOLF standing clear of the tank.

Standing just short of the vent can no longer do both at once.
- The relief vent's danger zone now matches its floor grate and the on-screen warning: any part of the engineer over the grate when it lets go is a knockdown, and the hint warns you while you stand there. The grate is drawn wider to show the whole zone.
- During a knockdown the engineer now draws in front of WOLF, and the vent's steam burst is lighter and see-through.
- The junction's pressure gauge now sits above the valve's label instead of covering it, and once the door is down the breaker and valve read SPENT, both right after the blast and after Continue.
- With REDUCED MOTION on, the charged door seam in the Service Junction glows steadily instead of strobing.
- Controller rumble now goes to the controller you are using, not always the first one connected.
- A damaged or hand-edited save that places the engineer or WOLF past the junction's sealed door is now rejected instead of loading into a spot with no way back.
- The play camera now sits closer to the engineer and WOLF in every room and shows them a little higher on screen, so their feet stay clear of the hint bar while the ceiling lamps and station labels remain in view. The Records and Service Junction closing shots move in closer instead of pulling back, without cutting any station label at the edge, and the first movement tip is centred over the room. Answer buttons sit a little higher and the junction's coolant tank hangs a little lower, so the tank stays in view below them when WOLF asks how to bring it down.

- In the Service Junction, a door that is already down now stays spent after Continue or a knockdown: using the breaker or the valve again no longer builds pressure, vents steam or sets off a second blast.

- The Service Junction's pipes no longer run through the OVERLOAD BREAKER and RELIEF VENT labels; the vent pipe now runs down to its floor grate, with its label beside it in the same font as the other labels.

- Returning to the title no longer leaves an unanswered choice that a later key press could answer.
- In-game changelog and credits links point at wolfoverride.mrdemonwolf.dev.
- Settings files are now read safely: damaged, oversized or unexpected content is ignored and default settings load instead.
- When Continue is unavailable, a note under it says whether no checkpoint exists yet or the saved checkpoint could not be read.
- When entering Records Access, copying a trace or securing the exit fails to save, the hint line now says so instead of a character's dialogue, and tells you to use a station to try again; normal hints return once you move on or a later save works.
- If the checkpoint file is missing or damaged, Continue uses a complete save that was still being written when the game closed, and Continue keeps working with saves made before small wording changes.
- Continuing in Records Access now tells you which copy is next or that the exit is open.
