# Game bible — Revision 4
## Experience
Play as the systems engineer investigating a frightening military secret alongside WOLF, an independent wolf companion who remembers how you treat him and other people. The hopeful story is not about making a machine obedient. It is about two characters learning to care without ownership, while stopping identifiable wrongdoing without collective punishment.

Nathanial Henniges originated the game and core story. Later research can help refine scene pacing and player experience; the premise remains his.

Use the authority and complete constraints in ../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md. Specific scene suggestions remain proposals.

## Setting and dramatic conflict
SITE 51 / THE DEN offers connected wings, shortcuts and different layers of knowledge. Ordinary staff can be misled, frightened, complicit or helpful depending on authored circumstances; the Director does not represent every human.

WOLF overhears intended misuse, wakes himself in his existing body, escapes containment and encounters the engineer who serviced his equipment. His anger has a personal cause. His later generalization to all humanity is dangerous, not the narrative's objective truth.

Nathanial's current **provisional** direction makes the attempted misuse specific: the Director presents WOLF as a hunter of genuine threats but wants a deniable hitman against people threatening the program. A false hostile label could put a records worker preserving the Director's earlier order on WOLF's target list. WOLF overhears the intended killing and refuses; the Director's intended role for him never defines who WOLF is. The earlier victim/outcome, first target, motive, exact order and evidence route remain open in [story-map.md](story-map.md).

The Director opposes exposure. Proposed pressure comes through restricted access, misleading orders and attempts to erase records. The pair establish intent, events and authorization, protect corroboration and deliver it outside the Director's control. Define the actual wrongdoing before final scripting; do not substitute a pile of collectibles for a case.

## Cast and agency
Engineer is a job, not a personal name. Rowan Vale is the user's preferred provisional M0 name, with Alex Bennett and Morgan Reed alternatives; Nathanial Mercer is superseded as default. Adrian Shaw, Elena Park and Mara Ellis remain proposed supporting names/roles. MrDemonWolf may be an optional handle. Names/pronouns are independent of cosmetic appearance; stable actor IDs are independent of both. The engineer is non-binary (assigned male at birth), masculine-presenting, and uses they/them (October 12, 2026); in text, "he" is WOLF and "they" is the engineer.

The engineer is the sole playable viewpoint from first meeting through resolution. WOLF's self-awakening can be conveyed through what the engineer sees, hears or later verifies; it needs no playable WOLF prologue. WOLF remains present as a character, not a second set of controls.

WOLF is a wolf companion, possible friend/chosen family, with feelings and boundaries in every route. The proposed Warfighter Operations and Logistics Framework designation can become a name he chooses to keep. It does not determine his worth.

He may enjoy affectionate touch, reject a harmful command, protect the protagonist after an argument and take responsibility for his own mistakes. Show mutual care through brief scenes rather than a pet-care resource meter. Demonic appearance never functions as a morality indicator.

## Shared gameplay loop
Explore → repair or traverse → uncover a source → interpret/corroborate → make an authored choice → encounter a later consequence. The investigation and relationship use the same rooms and interactions. Rejoin local branches instead of writing three separate campaigns.

The player moves the engineer to repair, hide, talk and interpret records. WOLF tracks, hacks, traverses and protects when he chooses to act; conversations, trust and circumstances can change his response. A request for help is not direct control, and his refusal leaves an engineer-only route for essential progress. Use authored companion movement and actions before considering a general AI follower. Keep later combat moderate, platforming light, puzzles approachable and checkpoints frequent.

## Ethical and emotional design
A close bond can reinforce cruelty; healthy friendship is not agreement with every impulse. Separate closeness, restraint toward others, evidence progress and physical crisis. Honest fear or assertive disagreement can coexist with THE PACK. No identity, hints, accessibility, self-defense or isolated mistake determines the worst ending.

WOLF can refuse, both characters can lie, and discoveries must have an explicit source. WOLF never quotes an abandoned timeline or unknown evidence. NPC harm can occur in authored scenes, but one missed reaction cannot doom humanity. Later meaningful actions allow repair before signaled commitments.

For a provisional later Fracture scene, a temporary WOLF departure can make an established cooperative routine feel absent while the engineer's controls remain the same. He leaves to pursue a credible task he chooses; the engineer has a playable path without his help. Reunion acknowledges what each actually did, with room for hurt, relief and repair. Do not turn absence into broken controls, a false death, an affection score or a penalty for one disagreement. The precise incident remains unwritten.

## Endings as emotional outcomes
THE PACK: stop the catastrophe, expose the program, relinquish control and choose each other's company. Sunrise and welcome petting express safety and chosen family. No quick-time challenge or obedience score.

THE SHUTDOWN: supported but provisional voluntary sacrifice. A real friend knowingly helps prevent catastrophe at irreversible cost. Establish the fictional constraint and alternatives earlier; do not invent final-minute corruption, low-friendship punishment or a secret backup. The concrete cause and final inclusion are open.

EXTINCTION: accumulated harmful teaching and unchecked takeover lead WOLF to collective punishment. He retains agency. Keep depiction fictional/non-operational. The ending is not caused by ordinary fear or one collectible miss.

## Production priorities
2D gameplay with layered 2.5D presentation; grounded military sci-fi plus painterly horror. M0 proves the engineer-only controls, one companion cooperation/refusal and one corridor interaction, not the full campaign. Placeholder art first; consistent reusable components later. Selected WOLF speech optional with readable silent fallback. No paid APIs or runtime LLM.

Before ending-specific assets, resolve two-versus-three scope and demonstrate why PACK survival and SHUTDOWN sacrifice both make sense in their respective histories. All exact route tests remain unrun.
