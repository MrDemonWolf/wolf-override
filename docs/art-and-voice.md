# Art, identity and voice — Revision 4
## Visual direction
Grounded military sci-fi plus dark painterly horror, layered side-view 2D. Reuse rooms/components rather than building a full 3D pipeline. WOLF evolves from sleek robot to demonic cyber-wolf; appearance never determines morality. He may retain his frightening appearance in THE PACK.

Small predefined human appearance roster; number/designs open. Reuse scale, baseline, uniform and animation layout. Name/pronouns independent of appearance/personality/morality. Current cast names and W.O.L.F. expansion remain candidates; see PROJECT_CONTEXT.md at the repository root. No art order is implied by a suggested name.

Current playable Rowan appearance direction (September 28, 2026): masculine-presenting adult using they/them pronouns. Rowan Vale remains a provisional name. Their field outfit is one cohesive set: deep crimson hooded utility jacket, charcoal tactical trousers and dark boots/gloves, with minimal cyan equipment light. Avoid yellow/ochre patches, bright blue fabric and shiny silver knee armor. The palette is a visual choice, not a military rank or faction claim.

For future app icons, preserve the existing [provisional wolf mark](../apps/game/assets/logo-mark.svg) and its [provenance record](../apps/game/assets/manifest.json). Prepare an editable 1024-square composition with an opaque full-bleed background and separate transparent wolf/ring SVG or PNG layers. Keep the wolf legible at small sizes and inside Android's [66/108 safe zone](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/system/icon_design_adaptive); leave platform masks, shadows and Apple Liquid Glass effects to the platform tools. Make an opaque flattened square for Windows and a distinct transparent foreground and monochrome silhouette for Android. [Apple Icon Composer](https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/) is the planned Mac/iOS layered source; IconWolf may make Android drafts, subject to visual review. Record every derivative and its rights status before use. No final icon artwork is approved.

## Companion acting and sunrise
WOLF can accept pet-like affection while remaining an independent friend. Show reciprocal care, not obedience or a care-resource meter. Proposed repair/limp/quiet-company moments use existing scenes. No petting-point grind or penalty for boundaries.

Adopted THE PACK payoff: sunrise outside THE DEN and the protagonist petting him. Proposed staging: offered hand, pause, WOLF approaching and resting his head beneath it; stroke behind an ear. He is free to leave and chooses to stay.

Draft human dialogue: “This okay?” Draft WOLF speech: “Yes.” Later: “I did not ask you to stop.” Unspoken notes: comfortable, quiet, dry humor. Human laughs and continues. Keep notes out of speech. The ending is earned before the scene; no timed prompt, skipped cinematic or missing voice can revoke it. Final animation is deferred beyond M0.

THE SHUTDOWN should portray losing a real friend, not discarding equipment. Proposed spoken line: “I trust you. Help me stop this.” Unspoken direction: deliberate and protective. Concrete cause, staging and final inclusion remain open; no ending assets should assume a finalized mechanism.

## Audio and provenance
Humans use written dialogue. Selected WOLF lines alone use optional TTS, evolving toward an androgynous synthetic/demonic direction. Always retain readable subtitles, mute and text fallback. Separate subtitle, spoken text and unspoken direction. Never block progress on speech timing/availability.

Verify actual voices, pronunciation and offline behavior for each tested target. Do not assume native TTS follows acting prompts or can be processed through Godot audio effects. No paid voice API or unreviewed cloning/redistribution. Name callbacks may stay written; no unwanted nickname abbreviations.

Use original placeholders and reusable 2D parts. Record source/tool, terms/license review, modifications and implementation status in `apps/game/assets/manifest.json`.
