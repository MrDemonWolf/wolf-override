# HQ batch: props and stations (Look 2, graphic-painterly)

Date: 2026-10-10. Folder: `scratchpad/hq/props/`. Nothing in the Git repository or any worktree was written. Nothing here was imported into Godot, rendered in the engine, played or device-tested. Every number below comes from the PNG files and `qa_asset.py`.

This file was rebuilt after the review pass. The QA and provenance table is now generated by `tools/notes_table.py` from `meta/*.json` and `qa/*.json`, and it checks each meta's `out_sha256` against the delivered PNG. The review sheets were re-rendered by `tools/review.py` from the current PNGs. Before delivery, all three sheets were rendered a second time to a temp folder and compared with the delivered sheets: 0.000% of pixels differed.

## What is delivered

- 29 final transparent PNGs at D = 4 (4 source px per world px). Of these, 28 were made in this batch, and `corridor-coolant-relay.png` is the approved style-test relay, reused unchanged:
  - 6 corridor and Records stations: purge display, breaker, coolant relay, safe point, purge queue and mirror port.
  - Junction (9):
    - the valve body, plus its wheel, gauge face and needle as separate stateful parts
    - the tank and the tank arm as separate sprites
    - the arm panel, the bolt panel and the relief vent (redesigned)
  - 6 pickups: data cartridge, lockout tag, cracked gauge, wheel ratchet, a work-order screen housing and a patrol-log screen housing. Both screen faces are dark.
  - 8 status-lamp sprites, one for each station socket the game toggles: breaker, relay, purge queue, purge display, mirror port, arm panel, bolt panel and relief vent. They are near-white, so code can tint them with `modulate`.
- `qa/<name>.json` for every sprite: `qa_asset.py`, family prop, `--scale 0.25 --zoom 1.35 --zoom 1.8 --baseline <row>`. **All 29 pass A-G.** No threshold was changed.
- `meta/<name>.json`: exactly one record per sprite. Each holds:
  - the raw source and its SHA-256
  - the matte mode, the downscale factor, the canvas, the offset and the baseline
  - every post-process step
  - the output SHA-256, which matches the delivered file for all 29
- `parts.json`: attach points in sprite pixels:
  - the wheel and gauge centres on the valve body
  - the shackle bottom on the arm and the lug top on the tank
  - each station's lamp socket rect and its lamp sprite
- Review sheets. Each asset is shown on mid-grey #808080 and on the approved style-test plate, at game size and at 2x:
  - `review-sheet-1-stations.png`
  - `review-sheet-2-junction.png`, with the assembled valve and arm+tank previews
  - `review-sheet-3-pickups-lamps.png`, with two lit-lamp previews
- "Game size" means the 1080p gameplay view of the style-test plate. Sprites are drawn at 2/3 of source size, the same scale the approved composite used, and the walk line is row 800. "2x" is that render enlarged with nearest neighbour.

## How it was made

- **Generation.** Every image came from Codex's built-in `image_gen` tool, through its imagegen skill, in edit mode with reference images and native transparent output:
  `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -s workspace-write -C raw/<job> --add-dir hq,art,style-test,art-audit`
  - No API key, CLI fallback or billed path was used.
  - All 22 Codex sessions were archived after their jobs finished. The ids are in `raw/<job>/session_id`.
- **References on every call:**
  1. The design reference or edit target: copies of `apps/game/assets/props/*.png` (identical to `art/props-junction/`), a kept master, or the previous raw of the same asset.
  2. `refs/ref-look2-board.png`: a composite of the approved style test, with composite-1920, a plate crop and the engineer, WOLF, breaker and relay cut-outs. It was built because the tool takes only five references.
  3. `refs/keyart-b.png` (title art B): mood and palette.
  4. `refs/palette-card.png` from the bible.
  5. An optional fifth reference: a layout guide (`refs/guide-relief-vent.png`), an extra identity image, or the finished `junction-valve.png` for steel values (the repair jobs).
- **Finishing.** `tools/process.py` follows bible sections 5.4 and 5.5:
  - It uses the native alpha and drops haze below alpha 16 and islands of fewer than 64 px.
  - Interior pixels become fully opaque.
  - Partial-alpha edge pixels take the colour of the opaque ring 2-4 px inside them, sampled the way check C samples it. This removes matte and spill.
  - It downscales once, in premultiplied linear light with Lanczos. Every source was downscaled at least 2.3x, and nothing was upscaled.
  - Canvases are multiples of 16 with 8 px of padding, and the baseline row is H-9. Rotating parts are centred on their pivot.
  - No sharpening.
- **Optional steps, per asset (listed in the table):**
  - **steel grade `--steel-grade CS,DL`:** a Lab grade on low-chroma pixels only. It applies fully below C* 23 and fades out by C* 27, so accents and lamps are untouched. It scales a*/b* by CS and adds DL to L*.
    - The junction set and the two purge consoles use **C* x0.65, L* -3**. This makes their steel match the low-chroma breaker and relay.
    - The cracked gauge uses **C* x1, L* +7**, which lifts its face to about L* 61.
  - **accent snap:** saturated pixels more than 10 dE from a bible swatch are pulled to within 10 dE of the nearest swatch, as the style test did.
  - **rim lift:** a dark 1-4 px silhouette rim is raised to the core L* minus 6. This removes dark outlines.
  - **top rim +N:** adds N to L* in the 1-4 px under a top-facing edge (a top light).
  - **cool b\* shift:** shifts low-chroma pixels toward blue. The current files no longer use it.
  - **3x3 median:** for display noise, as the style test did on WOLF.
  - **vertical fits:** a 1.076 vertical fit on the vent, and the lamp and needle fits.
- `tools/build_props.py` rebuilds and re-QAs every sprite from its selected raw (`work/extra_assets.json` holds the rest of the recipes).
- `tools/assemble_previews.py` rebuilds the assembled valve and arm+tank previews from the current sprites. It also re-measures the shackle and lug attach points against `parts.json`. `tools/lit_previews.py` rebuilds the lit-lamp previews. `tools/notes_table.py` writes the table below.

## Decisions, redos and deviations

  - Several finals therefore come from files Codex named `rejected-*`. I reviewed each one by eye. The reasons Codex gave were edge haze, fringe or canvas ratio, and the matte and resize step handles all of these.

  - The batch therefore applies `--steel-grade 0.65,-3` to: purge display, purge queue, valve body, valve wheel (hub), tank, tank arm, arm panel, bolt panel and relief vent.
