Use case: precise repair of an approved game sprite sheet (leg repaint only).
Image 1 is a 2 x 2 sheet of finished, approved paintings of WOLF, a robotic wolf, side view facing screen-right. In some figures one or two legs have been cut off below the body and replaced by flat grey mannequin legs (light grey = a NEAR leg, in front of the body; dark grey = a FAR leg, behind the body). Repaint ONLY those mannequin legs as WOLF's own fully rendered armoured legs, in exactly the mannequin's position and angle: the same upper-leg direction, the same joint bends, and the paw exactly where the mannequin's paw is. A mannequin paw resting on the ground line stays flat on that same ground line (sole level, all toes down); a mannequin paw drawn above the ground stays lifted by the same amount.
Each repainted leg must match WOLF's other legs in Image 1 exactly: the same plate shapes, thickness and length, the same round joint discs, the same armoured paw with its toe plates, and the same colours, values and painterly rendering. Join each repainted leg cleanly to the body at the shoulder or hip: where a piece of the old painted leg (an old elbow or knee joint, a plate edge) still shows at the cut, paint it over so each leg is one continuous leg following its mannequin. Keep the near/far layering: near legs in front of the body, far legs behind it.
Keep everything else exactly as it is in Image 1: the body, head, ears, eye, tail, plates, cyan seam lights, every other leg, every figure's size and position, and the finished figures that have no mannequin legs. Do not move, rescale or redraw any figure. Exactly four legs per figure. No flat grey mannequin shape may remain anywhere.
Rendering: clean graphic-painterly game sprite, NO dark outline around the figure, cold overhead light, a thin cyan-white rim light on top edges, fully opaque, no grain or speckle. Image 2 is WOLF's design sheet; Image 3 shows more finished frames of the same WOLF. Never redesign WOLF. No fur.
Background: keep the perfectly flat, uniform #FF00FF magenta background everywhere outside the figures. No floor, ground line, cast shadow, reflection, glow, gradient, text or borders. Never use magenta or pink on WOLF.
This sheet is a diagonal trot. In all four figures both HIND legs are mannequins; the front legs are finished, keep them exactly. Colour rule for the hind legs: the light grey mannequin hind leg is the NEAR hind leg (paint it with the pale silver-grey near-leg plates, in front of the body); the dark grey mannequin hind leg is the FAR hind leg (paint it with the darker blue-grey far-leg plates, behind the body). Do not swap them.
