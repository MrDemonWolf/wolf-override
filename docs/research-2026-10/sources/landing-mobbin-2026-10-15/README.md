# Landing page design brief, October 15, 2026

At Nathanial's request, one Codex chat used Mobbin to research how to make the public WOLF//OVERRIDE landing page (`apps/docs/app/page.tsx`) better. This is research only; nothing on the site has changed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `MOBBIN-LANDING.md` | The brief. It covers today's page and its weak spots, the ten highest-impact changes ranked, and the page structure for desktop and phone. It then gives section-by-section specs in Tailwind 4 terms; phone, performance and accessibility notes; what to avoid; the references; and open questions for Nathanial. |
| `references.json` | The 32 Mobbin references from 29 shipped products: product, screen, Mobbin link, pattern and what to borrow. |
| `queries.txt` | Every Mobbin search the chat ran. |
| `prompt.md` | The exact brief Codex was given. |

**Limits:**
- Mobbin holds few game-studio sites, so most references are film, streaming and product sites. The brief says so.
- Wording marked DRAFT needs Nathanial's approval before it goes on the site.
- Nothing was rendered or checked in a browser.
