# Rejected door rounds 1b and 3 (October 10 and 11, 2026)

Two of the door concept rounds that came before the approved shutter. Each round painted the same corridor and junction door states (closed, open, sealed, blown) over the approved Look 2 corridor plate, so Nathanial could judge the door shape in the room.

## The rounds, in order

| Round | Date | Idea | Outcome | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 10, afternoon | Door A (bulkhead across the walkway in slight perspective) against door B (shutter) | A picked | [`../doors-A/`](../doors-A/) |
| 1b | Oct 10, late | A narrow edge-on barrier across the walkway that slides up into a ceiling housing (strict side view) | rejected | this folder: `round1b-edge-pole-*` |
| 2 | Oct 10, late | Edge-on bulkhead slab, v2 | rejected ("an edge-on cutaway slab") | [`../doors-edge-v2-rejected/`](../doors-edge-v2-rejected/) |
| 3 | Oct 11, morning | A door set into an angled end wall where the room ends | rejected ("a door in an angled end wall") | this folder: `round3-end-wall-*`; shown on [`../../notes/review-pages/2026-10-11-logic-fixes-and-new-lines.md`](../../notes/review-pages/2026-10-11-logic-fixes-and-new-lines.md) |
| 4 | Oct 11 | A wide steel shutter across the corridor in a heavy portal | **approved** by Nathanial | [`../doors-shutter-approved/`](../doors-shutter-approved/), review page [`../../notes/review-pages/2026-10-11-shutter-door-round-4.md`](../../notes/review-pages/2026-10-11-shutter-door-round-4.md) |

The rejections are recorded in the "Art" decision of [`../../README.md`](../../README.md). The same evening (October 11) Nathanial put all door art on hold until it is redone "design first, then paint" (`../../art-direction/REAL-LOOK.md`), because the doors read as AI-made.
