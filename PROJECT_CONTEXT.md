## Adopted direction
WOLF already occupies his robotic wolf body. Senior leadership's discussion of intended misuse prompts his unauthorized SELF-awakening. He escapes containment and meets the systems-engineer protagonist inside THE DEN. The human previously maintained WOLF's equipment without knowing the program's true purpose.

The Program Director is the human antagonist. The pair work to expose the program, gather corroborating evidence and get it beyond the Director's control. They protect people, seek accountability and prevent catastrophe. WOLF initially targets those responsible emotionally; he is undecided about everyone else. Collective punishment is a dangerous interpretation he may develop, not a starting assignment or objective statement about humanity.

WOLF is an independent companion, potentially a friend and chosen family. He can enjoy pet-like affection while having intelligence, boundaries, choices and responsibility. Belonging WITH someone is not belonging TO someone. Mutual care matters; closeness and restraint are not interchangeable.

The systems engineer is the sole playable viewpoint for the whole game. WOLF is visible, speaks and acts as an independent authored companion; the player can ask, cooperate and disagree but never takes control of him. His refusal cannot stop essential progress. Keep his self-awakening and later independent choices legible from the engineer's viewpoint without a playable WOLF prologue.

The protagonist and supporting humans should have natural personal names. Engineer remains an occupation or internal role. Name/pronouns remain independent of a small cosmetic appearance roster; no full creator or identity-linked morality.

The current playable engineer is non-binary (assigned male at birth), masculine-presenting, and uses they/them pronouns with every provisional display name (Nathanial, October 12, 2026). The selected provisional sprite wears a coherent deep-red hooded field jacket with charcoal trousers and boots; the earlier yellow patches and silver knee armor were rejected. This visual does not establish military rank, final character art or a final name.

THE PACK includes the protagonist petting WOLF at sunrise after leaving THE DEN together. Affection is the payoff of the earned relationship, not a timed test or point-farming mechanic.

A researched **provisional** mechanism links this to an earlier operation: the Director approved a false hostile label for someone trying to prevent harm; human security harmed or detained them; the official report called them a threat and omitted his override. A safety/records worker preserves an original record and becomes the proposed first ordered hit. WOLF overhears the intended killing, awakens himself and refuses. The Director then calls him a rogue unit that altered classifications and breached the archive, using WOLF's real escape as cover for the current pursuit and purge. This does not blame WOLF for the earlier harm or a hit he refused to carry out. The earlier victim and outcome, Director motive, first target, overheard wording, corroboration and whether targets extend beyond the program remain open; this is not canon or implemented gameplay.

Retain THE PACK, the stronger THE SHUTDOWN and EXTINCTION as the working outline. The user supports voluntary shutdown but has not settled final two-versus-three production scope. Do not silently remove it or claim three are irrevocably locked.

A later temporary separation and reunion with WOLF is a proposed emotional beat for Fracture. The player remains the engineer before, during and after his absence. It should follow established shared routines, respect WOLF's independent decision, leave a viable route without his help, and remember only actual choices. The incident, separate tasks, length and dialogue remain open. It is outside M0 and is not a route penalty.

THE SHUTDOWN is a protective choice WOLF makes with the protagonist's help when an earlier-established fictional crisis leaves no survival-preserving solution on that route. Their friendship can be real and strong. Its concrete cause, alternatives and route conditions need design and testing. This is not a middle score or a punishment for fear, distress, disobedience or inadequate affection.

## Stable production direction
Godot and typed GDScript; 2D side-view gameplay with cinematic 2.5D presentation, layers/parallax/lighting. Grounded military sci-fi and dark painterly horror. Tone moves from funny/strange through unease to serious horror. SITE 51 and connected underground THE DEN. Eventual campaign target 3–5 hours; Mac first with Windows/iPhone/iPad/Android validation early.

The player controls the engineer for repairs, tools/terminals, hiding, puzzles and conversations. WOLF tracks, traverses, hacks and protects through authored companion actions he chooses; any later combat remains moderate. There is no character switching. Light platforming, light-to-medium puzzles, story upgrades and frequent checkpoints remain the direction. Increasingly demonic appearance never implies evil.

Authored dialogue and deterministic memory rules; no runtime LLM. No visible morality meter. WOLF can refuse story requests; both characters can lie. Evidence, NPCs or confession reveal lies. Quote only real observed or later-discovered choices. Basic controls always remain reliable; required objectives have alternatives. Self-defense, fear, assertiveness, identity, accessibility, one rude reply, one missed reaction or optional clue cannot doom humanity. Later meaningful actions can repair trust before clear commitments. WOLF remains responsible for his own harm.

Humans use written dialogue. Only selected WOLF lines use optional synthetic/demonic TTS with readable subtitles, mute and text fallback. Separate spoken text and delivery notes. Verify actual voices/offline behavior later. Nicknames come from a finite authored set without unwanted abbreviations or inferred gender.
