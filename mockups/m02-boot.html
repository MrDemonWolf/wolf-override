M02 Boot to title

What this page is for: judge the first two seconds of the game, from the launch image to a title you can use.

  • The screen above plays the boot once. Press Replay to see it again.
  • Press any key, click, tap or pad button while it plays to skip.
  • Pick a slow title load in the strip (Load) to see the sweep line.
  • Set Motion to reduced: the hop stops and the sweep becomes a slow pulse.
  • The title's backdrop is provisional key art (B13 review pending). Judge the boot on its timing and the logo, not on that picture.

What is a proposal here

  • Launch colour: the boot splash sits on the logo's navy, not black (5.2 step 1). Add splash=today to the URL for today's black.
  • One centred box for the logo (2.5.3 step 15), so it stays centred on wide phones.
  • The company name is part of the logo art, not reading text. It keeps the scene's own size (studio_logo.tscn CompanyName) on every device and TEXT SIZE, so the lockup keeps one shape and the sweep line never crosses it.
  • The sweep line is the shared indicator (5.8). It shows only when the whole wait passes ms (timing.indicator.quiet, 5.1), so a skip never brings a line and never makes the wait longer.
  • The title's entrance ends when the input guard ends, so most of it plays after the cover has cleared (principle 7: one orchestrated entrance).
  • The focus ring and the SELECT prompt wait for the input guard to end, so the screen never looks ready while it swallows presses.
  • Under REDUCED MOTION, the finished line reads differently from the pulse, and the line fades in as the game's does (studio_logo.gd BAR_IN_SECONDS).

Choices for Nathanial

  • Title backdrop: the key art is the first full-colour picture a new player sees, and it fails the AI-tell review (6.6 F, tells 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8). The floor is wet with no water, light streaks fall from the lamps for no reason, gloss and grime are even, and a front light on the engineer comes from no lamp. Keep it until B13, or build the title backdrop in M03 from the real corridor plates and sprites (data/corridor.json)?
  • The brand twice: the logo shows "MrDemonWolf, Inc.", and right after it the title says "MRDEMONWOLF, INC. PRESENTS" in another face and case. Drop that line when the title follows the logo (keep it after --skip-logo), or set it in the logo's face and case? Proposal: show both in M03 with a toggle. The build's strings do not change.
  • The logo's face: Montserrat is a common web face (1.7, "default fonts"). That question belongs to Q6.
