M06 Records Access choice

What this page is for: judge how the player answers WOLF at the mirror port, with every input.

  • Pad: X picks the first card, Y the second. The D-pad moves the ring; A picks the ringed card.
  • Keyboard: 1 and 2 pick a card. The arrow keys move the ring; Enter picks.
  • Mouse: hover lights one card; a click picks it on release.
  • Touch: the two cards fill the bottom row, in thumb reach; a tap picks one on release.
  • Each card shows the mark of who does the work: WOLF's mark on his readout, the engineer's badge on the manual port.
  • The question keeps the game's row rule: no row over the row length in languages.json.
  • The box sits at the top, placed by this page: at the bottom it would cover the cards (see "Top or bottom").
  • Try the strip: Input, TEXT SIZE and Speaker (placeholder portrait).

Not wired yet

  • A picked card only dips. The pair does not close, and nothing after the choice is shown.
  • On touch the box slides off the left edge. A shared touch rule moves it; the fix belongs in wo.css.
  • The shared rule does not pick top or bottom for a choice yet. It would keep the box at the bottom.
  • The machines' screens are dark: their lit glass is not drawn yet.

Answer card states

  • One frame per input, each pinned to it: keys and mouse, pad, touch.
  • Row 1: both cards resting.
  • Row 2 (mouse only): hover on the first card.
  • Row 3 (keys and pad): the ring on the first card.
  • Last row: the second card pressed.
  • Only one card is lit at a time.
