M06 Records Access choice

What this page is for: judge how the player answers WOLF at the mirror port, with every input.

  • Pad: X picks the first card, Y the second. The D-pad moves the ring; A picks the ringed card.
  • Keyboard: 1 and 2 pick a card. The arrow keys move the ring; Enter picks.
  • Mouse: hover lights one card; a click picks it on release.
  • Touch: the two cards fill the bottom row, in thumb reach; a tap picks one on release.
  • The box sits at the top here: WOLF, the engineer and the MIRROR PORT stand low in the view.
  • Try the strip: Input, TEXT SIZE, Speaker (placeholder portrait) and hit areas.

Not wired yet

  • X, Y, 1 and 2 do not pick a card here yet. They wait for the shared answer handler in wo.js.
  • A picked card does not close the pair yet. Nothing after the choice is shown.

Answer card states

  • Row 1: both cards resting.
  • Row 2: mouse hover on the first card.
  • Row 3: the ring on the first card, after a key or pad press.
  • Row 4: the second card pressed.
  • Only one card is lit at a time.
