M07 Pause and controller disconnected

What this page is for: check the pause card, its prompts, the disconnect card and the return from the background on each device.

1. Pause over the corridor

  • The world stops behind today's pause backdrop. At today's strength it reads only faintly.
  • Tune candidate for Nathanial: the backdrop's strength (today's is locked; for example 0.6 to 0.84).
  • The card opens with motion.screen.in: the backdrop fades in and the card rises.
  • RESUME holds focus. The ring shows only after a key or pad press.
  • Menu, B or Esc resumes. So does a click or tap on RESUME or on the footer's RESUME.
  • After resuming, Esc or Menu pauses again.
  • QUIT TO DESKTOP shows on desktop and ROG Ally (2023) only.
  • The warning sits right under RETURN TO TITLE, so it does not read as being about QUIT.
  • Question for Nathanial: should the same warning cover QUIT too? No new words until he decides.
  • Brief wording, not in the build yet: QUIT TO DESKTOP, SELECT, the disconnect card (4.5).

2. Return-to-title warning

  • Today's two warning lines sit under RETURN TO TITLE, on every pause.
  • No extra confirm step: it would add a press and new words.
  • Here RETURN TO TITLE holds focus.
  • This frame is pinned to pad so the focus is visible on every device.

3. Controller disconnected

  • Shows when the pad in use drops during play. The game pauses over the frozen corridor.
  • The card names every way back: reconnect, any key, or a tap.
  • Here B, Esc, Menu, or a click or tap anywhere on the card closes it.
  • Other keys and pad buttons do not close it in this mockup yet.
  • A reconnect alone does not resume; the next press does. A browser cannot simulate a reconnect.
  • Brief wording, not in the build yet: QUIT TO DESKTOP, SELECT, the disconnect card (4.5).

4. Back from the background (iPhone)

  • Always the iPhone 14 Pro Max, on touch.
  • The same pause card at touch density. No new text.
  • RESUME holds focus, with no ring on touch.
  • The world stays frozen behind the card, faint through today's backdrop.
  • Taps are ignored for timing.resume-card.guard after the page opens, which stands for the return.
