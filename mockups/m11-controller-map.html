M11 Controller map

What this page is for: see what every Xbox button does in each part of the game, and check that the map follows a remap.

  • Frames 1 to 4: the CONTROLLER MAP view, one per context.
  • Frames 5 to 7: Settings > CONTROLS, then one remap: Interact / continue moves to RT.
  • Below the frames: the button map table, the glyph families and the inputs we never use.
  • Device and input come from the strip. Sizes to judge: ROG Ally (2023) and desktop.
  • Waiting on shared: until the map renderer and binding remaps land, frames 1 to 4 and 7 show an empty map, and frame 6 still shows A.
  • Also waiting: the controller rows (frames 5 and 6) and the Keyboard column (section 9) follow the last input instead of showing pad buttons and keys.

Jump to: PLAY · DIALOGUE · MENUS · CREDITS · CONTROLS · after a remap · button map table · glyph families · never used

1 · CONTROLLER MAP: PLAY

  • Opened from Settings > CONTROLS (frame 5). Proposal.
  • Each label sits level with its button, so no line crosses another button.
  • Buttons with nothing to do here sit in one line under the pad. The Xbox button ends that line: the system keeps it.
  • D-pad left and right, or a click on the arrows, switch the context.

2 · CONTROLLER MAP: DIALOGUE

A conversation box is open. Walking and the menu buttons rest.

3 · CONTROLLER MAP: MENUS

Title, Pause and Settings. X and Y stay free in menus (Y is also Godot's ui_select).

4 · CONTROLLER MAP: CREDITS / CHANGELOG

Read-only pages: scroll, page and go back.

5 · CONTROLS: the way in

  • CONTROLLER MAP is the first row of the controller section, so a pad player finds it at once.
  • The binding rows show the bound glyph, read from the build's bindings (main.gd).

6 · CONTROLS after a remap: Interact / continue on RT

  • The player rebound Interact / continue to RT (RT does nothing in play, so there is no clash).
  • Menus still select with A: ui_accept is not remapped (C1 section 5).

7 · CONTROLLER MAP: PLAY after the remap

  • USE / HOLD TO CRANK now sits on RT.
  • A has nothing to do in play, so it joins the NOT USED HERE line.

8 · Button map by context (brief 4.2)

  • Every 4.2 row appears once in each context.
  • A callout with an action follows that action's binding; the others are fixed inputs.
  • Hold A to crank starts on the press frame; TOGGLE HOLDS latches it (6.7.1).

9 · Glyph families (brief 4.4)

  • The family comes from the pad's vendor ID first, its name second. Never the GUID.
  • Godot's buttons are positional: on a Nintendo pad the south button is printed B.
  • Keyboard glyphs show only after a key press. Touch shows no glyph: the on-screen control is the button.
  • PlayStation, Nintendo and Steam Deck are names until their glyphs exist (Q12: Kenney CC0 or painted).

10 · Never used (brief 4.1)

  • Stick clicks (LS and RS pressed in; L3 and R3).
  • Back paddles (Xbox Elite P1 to P4; Steam Deck L4, L5, R4, R5).
  • ROG Ally M1 and M2: system modifiers by default (ASUS).
  • Gyro, impulse triggers, the DualSense touchpad, Share and Capture.
  • Every screen works with the D-pad or stick plus A and B.
