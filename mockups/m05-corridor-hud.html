What this page is for: check the corridor HUD (box, bark, hint, pill, hit areas, touch controls, beacons) on every screen.

  • Pick a scene, device and input in the strip. Glyphs follow the input.
  • Speaker: name tab, colour and mark by default; "placeholder portrait" shows a labelled head crop (no portrait art exists, B24).
  • Phone controls: move pad and tap a station or WOLF by default; "tap to walk" hides the pad and USE.
  • Tick "hit areas" to see each tap area, each station's reach (solid) and its 0.1 s USE buffer (light; Proposal, 6.7.2).
  • FLASHING LIGHTS reduced turns the beacon pulse into a steady glow.
  • Row length: the build still wraps dialogue at 42 characters (languages.json en.row_characters) until B31 lands. The brief's 40 is a Proposal: add lang.en.row_characters=40 to the URL, or set it in Tune.

Conversation: types in at TEXT SPEED Normal; the triangle shows once the line is complete. Click the box to complete it, again to replay. The breaker's pill is hidden while the box is open.

The purge display at the left: its red screen is drawn by code in the build (prop_screen.gd) and not drawn here yet, so it shows dark.

Bark: a walk-and-talk line over WOLF. It never takes USE.

System hint: the same box with a terminal mark, never a second box.

Prompt pill: switch the input to keyboard, pad and touch. No keyboard glyph with a pad, none on touch.

Pointer hover out of reach: the relay is outlined and the pill says where a click walks to.

Walking: tick "hit areas" for every tap area and the reach bands. A tap area is cut back clear of the shown touch controls (6.4 step 13). One whose clear part is under the minimum turns red.

Hold View (Tab on a keyboard) to outline and name every usable thing, the locked SEAL too. Touch has no way to call this up yet (open question Q8).

Phone: the floor sits above the touch controls; the ring rests clear of the edge strip. Android: add cutout= and edge= (canvas px). A touch that starts on a station or WOLF uses it; anywhere else in the left 40% starts the pad (6.4 step 3: an object beats the floor).
